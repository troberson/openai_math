# 168 — Combinatorial invariance of Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomials

## Undergraduate summary

A **Coxeter system** is a group generated by reflections, with the
multiplication table of the reflections encoded in a matrix of
orders. From it, one builds the **Bruhat order** — a
partial order on the group elements — and intervals within
that order. For each interval, there is a polynomial called the
**Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomial** (1979), which encodes
deep geometric information (intersection cohomology of Schubert
varieties). The **combinatorial invariance conjecture** asks
whether this polynomial depends **only** on the isomorphism type
of the interval as a poset — that is, if two intervals (even
in different Coxeter systems) are order-isomorphic, do they carry
the same polynomial? This manuscript answers **yes**, for all
Coxeter systems, not just the classical types
[S3:51–53, S2:417].

## Graduate summary

**The main theorem** [S3:51–53, S4:9]. Every order
isomorphism $\iota: [u,b]^\wedge \simeq_o [u',b']^\wedge$
between Bruhat intervals in (possibly different) Coxeter systems
preserves the equal-parameter Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomial:
$P_{u,b} = P_{u',b'}$. The statement is **uniform** — no
restriction on the Coxeter systems (finite, affine, complex,
infinite), and the isomorphism is a bare poset isomorphism
[S3:51–53, S4:9].

**The equal-parameter form.** The result applies to the
**equal-parameter** Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomials, the
classical single-parameter family; the unequal-parameter
generalization (Hecke algebra deformations with two parameters)
is outside the statement
[S4:9].

**The scope of the formalization.** The formalization proves the
full combinatorial invariance theorem in its equal-parameter form
for arbitrary Coxeter systems, including the cross-system case
[S4:9].

## History of the conjecture

Kazhdan and Lusztig (1979) introduced their polynomials in the
study of Hecke algebras and intersection cohomology of Schubert
varieties. The **combinatorial invariance** question —
whether the polynomial is a function of the interval's order type
— was conjectured to hold broadly, and verified for the
classical finite Coxeter groups (symmetric groups, type $B$,
type $D$) and some affine cases. The **full** conjecture, for
arbitrary Coxeter systems including infinite and crystallographic
ones, remained open. This manuscript provides the general proof,
with a key reduction to dihedral (rank-2) intervals and a
localization argument
[S3:51–53, S2:417].

## Consequences

**The combinatorial invariance conjecture is resolved.** Equal-
parameter Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomials are determined by the
isomorphism type of the Bruhat interval as a poset, for all
Coxeter systems [S3:51–53, S4:9].

**Cross-system invariance.** The polynomial is the same even when
the two intervals live in different Coxeter systems
[S3:51–53, S2:417].

**The result is uniform.** No restriction on the Coxeter systems
— finite, affine, or infinite — and the isomorphism
is a bare poset order-isomorphism [S3:51–53, S4:9].
