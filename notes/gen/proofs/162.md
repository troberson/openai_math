# 162 — Counterexamples to Ryser's covering and Gyárfás's tree-cover conjectures

## Counterexamples to Ryser's covering conjecture

**Not formalized.** All three comparator targets are `sorry` stubs with no Lean implementation.

The preprint's main theorem [[2]] claims that for every sufficiently large prime q, there is a finite intersecting (q+1)-partite, (q+1)-uniform hypergraph H with equal part sizes |V_i| = q+1 and covering number τ(H) = q+1, with every vertex in an edge. Since ν(H) = 1, this gives τ(H) = r = q+1, contradicting Ryser's bound τ ≤ (r−1)ν.

The scope note [S1:10–12] records three results: balanced counterexamples at prime orders, fixed-prime counterexamples at ranks r = p^n + 1, and odd extension degree results. All three comparators are `sorry` stubs:

- `BalancedRyser.lean` [S2:20–21] [[1]]: `balanced_ryser_counterexample := by sorry`.
- `RyserCovering.lean`: `ryser_counterexample := by sorry` (60-line stub).
- `RyserOddExtensions.lean`: `ryser_odd_extensions := by sorry` (75-line stub).

No Lean files exist under `lean/OAI/` for any of the three comparators, and no `main_results` entries exist.

**Scope note overclaim:** the scope note [S1:10] states "The formalization proves that every sufficiently large prime q has a finite intersecting (q+1)-partite, (q+1)-uniform hypergraph" — but the comparator is a `sorry` stub. The statement is declared as a `Prop` and the theorem is left unproven.
