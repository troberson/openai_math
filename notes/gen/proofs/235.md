$`\alpha_k\in(0,\infty)`$ such that, for independent proper uniformly
signed $`k`$-clauses sampled with replacement,
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}P_n(\lfloor cn\rfloor)
 =
 \begin{cases}
  1,&0\le c<\alpha_k,\\
  0,&c>\alpha_k.
 \end{cases}$$ [S5:31–43] states the

prove the variance bounds $`\mathrm{Var}(H_n)=\Theta_k(n)`$ for $`k\ge 4`$
and the computability of $`\alpha_3`$.
