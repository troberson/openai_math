## Weak Hessian bounds along every geodesic in RCD spaces

**Match.** The manuscript's headline [S2:15]{2} is the weak Hessian bound along every geodesic: on a full-support RCD(K,N) space, a bounded globally-Lipschitz F with a bounded weak Hessian in the distributional sense has its Hessian bounded along every geodesic. The Lean carrier `every_geodesic` [S3:38]{3} proves exactly this on a complete separable RCD(K,N) space. The scope note [S1:9]{1} records the same alignment. Zero sorry in the closure.
