## Geodesic regularity under weak Hessian bound

**Match.** The scope note The formalization transfers a weak Hessian upper bound to every prescribed minimizing geodesic in an $`\mathrm{RCD}(K,N)`$ space with finite $`N>1`$. If a bounded globally Lipschitz function $`F`$ has weak Hessian bounded above by $`G`$ times the metric, where $`G`$ is bounded and continuous, then every constant-speed geodesic $`\gamma:[0,1]\to X`$ satisfies $`(F\circ\gamma)''\le G(\gamma)\,d(\gamma(0),\gamma(1))^2`$ in the distributional sense. Constant geodesics are included. The space is complete and separable with full support and measure finite on bounded sets; compactness and metric nonbranching are not assumed.
