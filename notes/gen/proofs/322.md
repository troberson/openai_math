# 322 — Tingley's sphere-isometry problem

## Tingley's problem for the sphere: every surjective isometry extends to a linear one

**Match.** The manuscript's headline [S2:51] is Tingley's problem in the affirmative: every distance-preserving bijection of the unit sphere extends to a linear isometry of the ambient space. The Lean carrier `tingley_sphere_isometry` [S3:95] proves exactly this in the real Banach-space setting: a surjective isometry of the unit sphere (norm 1 elements) extends to a unique bounded linear operator mapping the ambient space onto itself. The scope note [S1:9] records the same alignment. Zero sorry in the closure.
