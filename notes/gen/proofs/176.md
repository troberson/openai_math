# 176 — The second Kahn–Kalai conjecture

## The second Kahn–Kalai conjecture with an edge-count bound

**Match.** The single comparator target is proven as a `main_results` entry.

The preprint's main theorem [[2]] states that for every n ≥ 2 and every finite simple graph H with h = e(H) ≥ 1 edges and at most n vertices, pc(n,H) ≤ min{1, 2048·e⁵⁰·𝔼(n,H)·(1+log₂ h)}, and in particular pc(n,H) ≤ 6144·e⁵⁰·𝔼(n,H)·log₂ n.

The Lean declaration `secondKahnKalaiBounds` [S2:25–27] [[1]] proves `SecondKahnKalaiBounds n H` for any n ≥ 2, finite type V with |V| ≤ n, and simple graph H with at least one edge. The proof (lines 28–50+) constructs a spread orbit tree via `exists_spread_orbit_tree`, derives a tree sampling parameter pTree with explicit constants (16·e⁵⁰·128q·(1+log₂ m)), and composes the tree covering and monotonicity lemmas. The scope note [S1:9] records the same: universal factor times 1+log₂|E(H)|, and hence up to a universal factor times log₂ n. The single comparator [S1:15] (`SecondKahnKalai.lean`) is the shared target.
