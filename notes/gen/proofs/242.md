## Single-fold Diophantine representations

**Match.** The scope note The single-fold Diophantine conjecture asks whether every recursively enumerable set has a polynomial representation with a unique auxiliary witness for each member. The formalization establishes this for every recursively enumerable subset of $`\mathbb N^n`$, $`n\ge1`$: an integer polynomial has exactly one complete natural-number witness tuple for members and none for nonmembers.
