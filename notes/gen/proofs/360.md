## Global support and convex injectivity domains under weak MTW

**Match (2 of 2).** The scope note On a compact connected smooth Riemannian manifold of dimension at least two, the formalized result proves that weak MTW curvature implies convexity of every open tangent injectivity domain. Prior convexity or nonfocality is not assumed.
