# 119 — The Courtade–Kumar and Hellinger conjectures

## statement

The preprint's main theorem (sharp soft-channel contraction):

> **theorem**
For every finite $`n\ge0`$, every $`u:\Omega_n\to[-1,1]`$, and
$`-1\le\rho\le1`$,
$$I(T_\rho u)
 \le\psi\!\left(|\rho|\,\psi^{-1}(I(u))\right).$$
The inverse is the nonnegative increasing branch on $`[0,L]`$.
For $`n\ge1`$, every soft coordinate $`u(x)=a x_i`$, $`|a|\le1`$,
attains equality. [S1:72–84]

The Lean proves the Boolean corollary (Courtade–Kumar) plus attainment
via the `FullStatement` carrier:

```lean
theorem courtadeKumarAndAttainment : FullStatement :=
```
[S2:60]

The scope note spells out the Boolean bound:

> For Boolean functions this includes the Courtade–Kumar inequality $`I(f(X);Y)\le1-h_2(\varepsilon)`$ in bits at crossover probability $`0\le\varepsilon\le1/2`$, where $`h_2`$ is binary entropy. A coordinate and its complement attain equality. [S3:11]

## the match

The `courtadeKumarAndAttainment` declaration [S2:60] is a `FullStatement`
pairing two components:

1. **`courtadeKumar : CourtadeKumarStatement`** [S2:56] — the bound
   $`I(f(X);Y) \le 1 - h_2(\varepsilon)`$ for Boolean functions $`f`$ on the
   $`n`$-cube at crossover probability $`\varepsilon \in [0, 1/2]`$.

2. **`dictatorAttainment`** — the equality case: a coordinate and its
   complement attain the bound.

The preprint's Theorem 1 [S1:72–84] is the **soft** (real-valued)
contraction: $`I(T_\rho u) \le \psi(|\rho| \psi^{-1}(I(u)))`$ for all
$`u : \Omega_n \to [-1,1]`$, with coordinate attainment. The Boolean
bound [S3:11] is a **corollary** obtained by restricting to
$`\{0,1\}`$-valued functions: the soft contraction specializes to
$`I(f(X);Y) \le 1 - h_2(\varepsilon)`$.

The Lean's `courtadeKumar_bound` [S2:47–54] proves the Boolean form
directly via `mutualInformation_eq_informationDeficit` and
`informationDeficit_noise_le_psi`, which is the soft contraction
applied to the sign encoding of a Boolean function.
