# 227 — The dynamical phase transition in the Sherrington–Kirkpatrick model

## Critical SK dynamics and the temperature transition

**Partial (2 of 5).** The scope note makes five claims over the
high-temperature spectral gap For every fixed inverse temperature $`0<\beta<1`$, the formalization proves a dimension-independent Poincaré inequality for the zero-field Gaussian Sherrington–Kirkpatrick model with probability tending to one over the disorder. Equivalently, the unscaled single-site heat-bath spectral gap stays bounded away from zero. For dynamics that choose one site uniformly at each discrete step, the spectral gap is at least $`1/(Cn)`$ with probability tending to one, for a constant $`C`$ depending on $`\beta`$. [S1:12], ratio cutoff
The formalized supporting result proves ratio cutoff for discrete single-site heat-bath dynamics in the zero-field Gaussian Sherrington–Kirkpatrick model for $`0\le\beta<1/2`$. For every $`0<\varepsilon<1/2`$ and $`\eta>0`$, the probability over the disorder that $`t_{\mathrm{mix}}(\varepsilon)/t_{\mathrm{mix}}(1-\varepsilon)>1+\eta`$ tends to zero as $`n\to\infty`$. The denominator is positive for all sufficiently large sizes. [S1:14], critical mixing bounds, covariance/Rayleigh estimates,
and stretched-exponential barriers. Two comparators have `main_results`
entries with OAI proofs: `CriticalSKMixing`
(`OAI.CriticalSK.critical_mixing_bounds`, in `CriticalMixing/Main.lean`,
51 lines, 0 `sorry`) and `CriticalSK`
(`OAI.CriticalSK.covariance_and_linear_tests`, in
`CriticalSK/Rayleigh.lean`, 627 lines, 0 `sorry`). The three
comparators `SKHighTemperature`, `SKRatio`, and `SKBarriers` have no
`main_results` entries; their stubs are `sorry`.

The manuscript's spectral-gap theorem **theorem**
There is a finite constant $`C_\beta`$, depending only on $`\beta`$, such that
$$\mathbb P_J\left\{\mathrm{Var}_{\mu_0}(f)\le C_\beta\mathcal D_0(f)
        \text{ for every }f:\{-1,1\}^n\longrightarrow\mathbb R\right\}
  \longrightarrow1.$$ [S4:33–39] states the
Poincaré inequality $`\mathrm{Var}_{\mu_0}(f)\le C_\beta
\mathcal{D}_0(f)`$ with probability tending to one. The Lean proof
covers the critical mixing bounds and the covariance/Rayleigh
estimates; the high-temperature spectral gap, the ratio cutoff, and
the stretched-exponential barrier results are not formalized.
