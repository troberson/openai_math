# 174 — Deterministic construction of strong thin spanning trees

## The strong thin-tree conjecture

**Partial.** 1 of 2 comparator targets is proven as a `main_results` entry.

The preprint's main theorem [[2]] claims a universal constant C > 0 such that every finite k-edge-connected loopless multigraph with at least two vertices has a spanning tree T with |δ_T(S)| ≤ (C/k)|δ_G(S)| for every nontrivial cut S.

The Lean declaration `strongThinTree` [S2:24–28] [[1]] proves `MainStatement`: ∃ C > 0, ∀ k ≥ 1, ∀ n ≥ 2, every k-edge-connected loopless multigraph G on n vertices admits a spanning tree T with |δ_T(S)| ≤ (C/k)·|δ_G(S)| for all nontrivial cuts. The proof assembles `MultiGraph.packing_thinness` to get a base constant C₀, sets C = 3·max(C₀, 1), and splits on k ≥ 2 vs k = 1.

The scope note [S1:10] records the existential bound without a numerical value of C or a construction algorithm. The `AlgorithmicThinTrees` comparator [S1:19] has **no `main_results` entry** — the deterministic polynomial-time construction with binary-encoded parallel edges is not a standalone declaration in the formalization.
