## Kadison's similarity conjecture

**Not formalized.** The scope note claims Kadison's similarity problem asks whether every bounded unital homomorphism from a unital complex $`C^*`$-algebra into operators on a Hilbert space is similar to a $`*`$-homomorphism. The formalization proves this for arbitrary Hilbert spaces. It also gives one universal hyperreflexivity constant for all unital von Neumann algebras. [S1:9] that

The manuscript's main theorem **theorem**
Let $`A`$ be any unital complex $`C^*`$-algebra, let $`H`$ be any complex
Hilbert space, and let $`\pi:A\to\mathcal B(H)`$ be a bounded,
complex-linear, unital algebra homomorphism. There exists an operator
$`S\in\mathcal B(H)`$, invertible with bounded inverse, such that
$$\rho(a)=S\pi(a)S^{-1}\qquad(a\in A)$$
is a $`*`$-homomorphism. In particular,
$`\rho(a^*)=\rho(a)^*`$ for every $`a\in A`$. [S4:15–25] states Kadison's
similarity problem in full generality: every bounded unital homomorphism
from a unital complex $`C^*`$-algebra into $`\mathcal{B}(H)`$ is similar to
a $`*`$-homomorphism, with no separability, nuclearity, or finite
generation condition. The companion result gives a universal
hyperreflexivity constant for all unital von Neumann algebras.
