# 140 — Memory–sample lower bounds for noiseless Gaussian regression

## Memory and precision in noiseless Gaussian regression

**Partial.** 5 of 8 comparator targets are proven as `main_results` entries (7 declarations total); 3 comparators carry no entry.

Family 140 spans 6 manuscripts and 8 comparators. The scope note [S1:7–30] covers the full web: memory-precision bounds, replica equalities, moment decompositions, replacement theorems, and subsphere current estimates.

**Proven (5 comparators, 7 declarations):**

- **MemoryPrecision** [S2:59–73]: a nine-conjunct theorem [[1]]. The final conjunct carries the paper's main bound: for d ≥ d₀, M ≤ d², ε ≤ 1/10, any learner with success probability ≥ 2/3 requires T ≥ c·d·log(1/ε). This matches the preprint's Theorem h:main [[7]].
- **PosteriorReplicas** [S3:19–25]: an eight-conjunct theorem [[2]] covering equal-label identities, Gaussian/Distance/Haar/Synthetic/Incidence replica blocks, and the subquadratic memory claim.
- **ProjectionMoments** [S4:14–26]: a fifteen-conjunct theorem [[3]] covering integrated frame moments, sphere moments, Riesz offsets, Haar/Riesz kernel success, cap bounds, subquadratic memory, and fixed quadratic memory.
- **GaussianReplacement (hybrid)** [S5:97–101]: the hybrid block bound with explicit numeric constants [[4]].
- **GaussianReplacement (fiber)** [S6:74–76]: the fiber block bound with explicit numeric constants [[5]].
- **SubsphereCurrent** [S7:15–18]: a six-conjunct theorem [[6]] covering explicit precision, radius-weighted full estimate, half precision, affine full estimate, dimension estimate, and mixture estimate.

**Not in `main_results` (3 comparators):**

- **NoiselessRegression** [S1:43]: no `main_results` entry. The learner theorems are used as lemmas inside the ProjectionMoments and PosteriorReplicas declarations above.
- **GaussianFiniteEntropy**: no `main_results` entry. The replacement bound is a lemma inside the GaussianReplacement declarations.
- **GaussianInformation**: no `main_results` entry. The block bound is a lemma inside the fiber and hybrid declarations.

This is a **partial** result: the three comparators without `main_results` entries are not standalone theorems in the formalization but are subsumed as lemmas by the declarations that are proven.
