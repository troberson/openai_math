# 197 — Nonsofic groups and group-ring counterexamples

## A torsion-free group algebra that is not directly finite

**Match.** Three OAI declarations prove three of the four sub-claims, all with 0 `sorry`: `OAI.GroupRingDeterminant.main` (the group-ring determinant conjecture, `OAI/Analysis/GroupDeterminants/Main.lean`, 17 lines), `OAI.KaplanskyCounterexample.finitelyPresented_counterexample` (`OAI/RingTheory/DirectFiniteness/FinitelyPresented.lean`, 44 lines), and `OAI.OddKaplansky.main_theorem` (`OAI/Algebra/OddKaplansky/Main.lean`, 110 lines).
