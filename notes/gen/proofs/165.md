# 165 — Exact crossing numbers of complete and complete bipartite graphs

## The Harary–Hill and Zarankiewicz crossing-number formulas

**Not formalized.** Both comparator targets are `sorry` stubs with no Lean implementation.

The preprint's main theorem [[2]] states Hill's formula: for every n ≥ 3, cr(K_n) = ¼·⌊n/2⌋·⌊(n−1)/2⌋·⌊(n−2)/2⌋·⌊(n−3)/2⌋. The scope note [S1:10] records the lower bound and the matching two-page drawing. The second result (Zarankiewicz's formula for cr(K_{m,n})) is at [S1:12].

Neither comparator has a `main_results` entry. The two stubs carry the target statements:

- `CompleteCrossing.lean` [S2:82–83]: `ordinaryCrossingNumber (completeGraph order) = hill order := by sorry` [[1]].
- `BipartiteCrossing.lean`: a 68-line `sorry` stub for the bipartite crossing number.
