# 207 — The $\ell^1$-Bass and complex Bass trace conjectures

## The Bass trace conjecture for complex group rings

**Match.** The manuscript's headline [S2:42] is the complex Bass trace conjecture for every discrete group: the Hattori–Stallings trace of a finitely generated projective module over the complex group ring is supported on conjugacy classes of finite-order elements. The Lean carrier `bassTraceModules_vanishing_and_support` [S3:23] proves exactly this: for any group G and any virtual class x, the trace vanishes on infinite-order conjugacy classes and the support is contained in the finite-order classes. The scope note [S1:9] records the same alignment. Zero sorry in the closure.
