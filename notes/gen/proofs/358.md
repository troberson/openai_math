## A three-manifold without conjugate points and without nonpositive curvature

**Match.** The manuscript's headline [S2:27]{2} is the existence theorem: a closed connected orientable smooth three-manifold M with a smooth Riemannian metric without conjugate points, such that M admits no smooth Riemannian metric with sectional curvature everywhere at most zero. The Lean carrier `main_theorem : MainStatement` [S3:260]{3} proves this: the real compact three-manifold with the constructed metric has no conjugate points and no nonpositive-curvature metric.
