# 172 — Classification of finite Euclidean Ramsey configurations

## Classification of finite Euclidean Ramsey configurations

**Not formalized.** All seven comparator targets are `sorry` stubs with no Lean implementation.

The preprint's classification theorem [[2]] states that a finite configuration A ⊂ ℝᵈ (at least two points, affine span ℝᵈ) is Euclidean Ramsey — every finite coloring of some high-dimensional Euclidean space contains a monochromatic congruent copy at the original scale — if and only if there is a matrix P ∈ Mat_{d+1}(B) satisfying the tensor-field evaluation and field-spatial equations.

The scope note [S1:9–11] records seven results: the tensor-field classification, singleton and affine-span reductions, cosphericity, the Ramsey property for subsets of transitive configurations and for at most five circle points, the quadratic-independence criterion, and the two spherical non-Ramsey examples (twelve-point and nine-point).
