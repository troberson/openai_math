# 107 — Matrix multiplication with exponent at most $9/4$

## statement

The preprint's main theorem:

> **theorem**
Over $`\mathbb C`$, the arithmetic matrix-multiplication exponents satisfy
$$\omega<\frac{1129}{500}=2.258,\qquad
 \alpha>0.465,\qquad w(0.709)<2.092.$$ [S1:73–78]

The Lean proves three separate complex-matrix bounds, each with the
quantitative constant verbatim in its statement:

## the square-bound constant

The load-bearing nuance is the square exponent. The Lean proves
`Arithmetic.omega ℂ ≤ 9/4` [S2:15], i.e. $`\omega \le 2.25`$. The scope note
[S4:10] describes the formalization as "gives $`\omega(\mathbb{C}) \le 9/4`$".
The preprint's headline, however, is $`\omega < 1129/500 = 2.258`$ [S1:76].

Since $`9/4 = 2.25 < 2.258 = 1129/500`$, the Lean's bound is **stronger**
than the preprint's: `ω ≤ 9/4` implies the preprint's `ω < 2.258`. This is
not a contradiction — the formalization **implies** the paper's claim — but
the formalized constant ($`9/4`$) is not the paper's headline constant
($`1129/500`$). The scope note reports the formalized constant, not the
paper's.

| Bound | Preprint | Lean | Scope note | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| square $`\omega`$ | `$`< 1129/500 = 2.258`$` [S1:75] | `$`\le 9/4 = 2.25`$` [S2:15] | `$`\le 9/4`$` [S4:10] | **Match (Lean stronger; implies preprint)** |
| dual $`\alpha`$ | `$`> 0.465`$` [S1:76] | `$`93/200 = 0.465 <`$ complexAlpha` [S2:20] | `$`> 0.465`$` [S4:10] | **Match** (exact) |
| rectangular $`w(0.709)`$ | `$`< 2.092`$` [S1:76] | `$`< 523/250 = 2.092`$` [S2:26] | `$`< 2.092`$` [S4:10] | **Match** (exact) |
| all-fields $`\omega(F)`$ | — (second manuscript) | `$`< 2.371054886006746`$` [S3:18] | `$`< 2.371…`$` [S4:12] | **Match** (exact constant) |

The dual and rectangular bounds are exact matches: $`93/200`$ is $`0.465`$, and
$`523/250`$ is $`2.092`$. The all-fields bound carries the full-precision
constant. Only the square bound uses a different (stronger) constant than
the preprint's headline.

## findings

- Square $`\omega(\mathbb{C})`$: **Lean stronger** — $`\le 9/4 = 2.25`$ implies
  the preprint's $`< 1129/500 = 2.258`$; constants differ, not directions.
- Dual $`\alpha`$: **matches** exactly ($`93/200 = 0.465`$).
- Rectangular $`w(0.709)`$: **matches** exactly ($`523/250 = 2.092`$).
- All-fields $`\omega(F) < 2.371054886006746`$: **matches** — full-precision
  constant, with the $`< 2.371056`$ companion.
- Proof-level faithfulness: **no divergence**; the only constant difference
  (square) is a strengthening, so the Lean implies the preprint's claim.
