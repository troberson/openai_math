# 087 — The Mahler conjectures and symplectic width

## The Mahler conjectures, functional inequalities and polar-product symplectic width

**Partial.** 3 of 4 comparator targets are proven as `main_results` entries.

Family 087 spans 3 manuscripts and covers the symmetric and general Mahler conjectures plus the symplectic ball capacity of symmetric polar products.

**Proven (3 comparators):**

- **MahlerConjecture** [S2:12–22] [[1]]: the symmetric Mahler inequality |K|·|K°| ≥ 4ⁿ/n! for every origin-symmetric convex body K ⊂ ℝⁿ, n ≥ 1. This matches the preprint's main theorem [[4]].
- **SymmetricMahlerEquality** [S3:534–540] [[2]]: equality holds if and only if K is a linear Hanner body. The biconditional (volume product = 4ⁿ/n!) ↔ IsLinearHanner K.
- **SymmetricPolar** [S4:7–15] [[3]]: the Gromov width of the symmetric polar product is exactly 4, and every ball of capacity 0 < c < 4 embeds symplectically. This matches the preprint's theorem [[6]].

**Not in `main_results` (1 comparator):**

- **GeneralMahler** [S1:15–17]: the general (nonsymmetric) Mahler conjecture P(K) ≥ (n+1)^{n+1}/(n!)² with simplex equality is the subject of the second manuscript [[5]], but has no `main_results` entry and is a 39-line `sorry` stub.
