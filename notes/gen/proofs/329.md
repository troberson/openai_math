# 329 — A counterexample to Pietsch's metric-entropy duality conjecture

## A counterexample to metric-entropy duality

**Match.** The manuscript's headline [S2:30]{2} is that for every a, b ≥ 1, there is a positive integer n and an origin-symmetric convex body K in R^n such that the covering number of K is strictly larger than the corresponding polar covering number bound raised to the b-th power. The Lean carrier `exists_entropy_duality_counterexample` [S3:72]{3} proves exactly this: for a, b ≥ 1, there exists n > 0 and a symmetric convex body K whose log covering number exceeds b times the log polar covering number. The scope note [S1:9]{1} records the same alignment. Zero sorry in the closure.
