# 328 — Fixed points of nonexpansive maps in reflexive Banach spaces

## Nonexpansive fixed points in reflexive Banach spaces

**Match.** The manuscript's headline [S2:9] is the nonexpansive fixed-point theorem for reflexive spaces: a nonexpansive map on a nonempty, norm closed, bounded, convex subset of a real reflexive Banach space has a fixed point. The Lean carrier `hasNonexpansiveFixedPointProperty_of_canonicallyReflexive` [S3:26] proves exactly this for canonically reflexive spaces: every nonexpansive self-map of a nonempty closed bounded convex set has a fixed point. The scope note [S1:9] records the same alignment. Zero sorry in the closure.
