# 157 — Counterexamples to the Hadwiger and Colin de Verdi\`ere conjectures

## Hadwiger's conjecture counterexample and list-coloring bound

**Partial.** 1 of 2 comparator targets is proven as a `main_results` entry.

The preprint's main theorem [[2]] claims arbitrarily large m-vertex finite simple graphs G with α(G) ≤ 2 and cm(G) < m/100, which via the counting inequality h(G) ≤ (|V(G)| + 4·cm(G) + 2)/3 gives h(G) < m/2 ≤ χ(G), disproving Hadwiger's conjecture.

The Lean declaration `not_hadwiger_conjecture` [S2:120–123] [[1]] is the negation of the universal Hadwiger claim: ¬(∀ m, G, χ(G) ≤ h(G)). The proof assembles `exists_counterexamples 0` to produce a specific counterexample graph G with the required properties, then derives the contradiction.

The scope note [S1:10–14] records two results: the arbitrarily large counterexamples (proven above) and the Linear List Hadwiger bound (∃ C ≥ 1, ∀ G, χ_list(G) ≤ C·h(G)). The ListHadwiger comparator [S1:20] has **no `main_results` entry** — it is not a standalone declaration in the formalization. The HadwigerCounterexample comparator [S1:21] is proven.
