# 195 — A counterexample to the small Cohen–Macaulay module conjecture

## A complete local domain without a small Cohen-Macaulay module

**Partial.** The manuscript's headline [S2:20] has two parts: (a) existence of a three-dimensional complete Noetherian normal local domain over C with residue field C, and (b) that domain has no nonzero finitely generated maximal Cohen-Macaulay module. The Lean carrier `candidate_ring_properties` [S3:13] proves part (a) exactly: the completed surface cone is a domain, Noetherian, integrally closed, local, over C, adic-complete, dimension 3, with bijective residue map. Part (b) (the absence of small CM modules) is the conjecture-disproving claim, and the scope note [S1:9] records that the formalization carries the ring construction. The "no max CM module" property is not in the Lean carrier statement. Zero sorry in the closure for what is proved.
