# 113 — Approximate counting and the perfect-matching entropy conjecture

## statement

Family 113 ships two preprints with two main theorems:

> **theorem**
There is a uniform classical randomized algorithm which, given $`G`$ and
rational parameters $`0<\varepsilon<1`$ and $`0<\delta<1/2`$, returns a nonnegative
rational number $`\widehat Z`$ such that
$$\mathbb P\bigl[(1-\varepsilon)Z(G)\le \widehat Z\le(1+\varepsilon)Z(G)\bigr]
 \ge 1-\delta.$$
If $`Z(G)=0`$, the output is zero with certainty. The worst-case bit running
time is polynomial in the input encoding length, $`\varepsilon^{-1}`$, and
$`\log\delta^{-1}`$. [S1:132–143]

> **Theorem 1.1**
Let $`G`$ be a loopless multigraph on $`2m\ge2`$ vertices with at least one
perfect matching. For every $`x\in P(G)`$,
$$F(x)-\left(2-\frac2m\right)B(x)
                  \le H(x)\le F(x).$$ [S2:33–40]

Neither main theorem is a `main_results` entry. The three declared
declarations are secondary results:

## the entropy bound: old vs refined

The proven `entropy_main` [S3:85–89] states:

$$\text{marginalEntropy}(y) - H(y) \le 8m\bigl(1 - e^{-\text{marginalEntropy}(y)/m}\bigr)$$

The preprint's main theorem [S2:33–40] states:

$$F(x) - \bigl(2 - \tfrac{2}{m}\bigr) B(x) \le H(x) \le F(x)$$

These are **different bounds**. The Lean's coefficient-8 bound is the
earlier, weaker result that the preprint *retains* (scope note [S6:14]:
"It retains the earlier coefficient-eight entropy bound"). The refined
pointwise bound with the $`(2 - 2/m)`$ factor is in the comparator stub
`MatchingEntropyBounds`, not in `main_results`.

The scope note's sentence "The formalization proves
$`F(x)-(2-2/m)B(x)\le H(x)\le F(x)`$" [S6:14] is **inaccurate**: the
`main_results` entry proves the coefficient-8 bound, not the refined
pointwise bound. The refined bound is a comparator-stub target, not a
proved declaration.
