## Riesz transforms and uniform rectifiability in higher codimension

**Match.** The manuscript's headline [S2:20]{2} is: an n-Ahlfors–David regular Radon measure on ℝ^d is uniformly n-rectifiable if its Riesz transform is uniformly bounded in L², for d ≥ 4 and 2 ≤ n ≤ d−2. The Lean carrier `QuantitativeFullStatement` [S3:42]{3} states exactly this: the dimension range, AD-regularity hypothesis, Riesz L² boundedness hypothesis, and the BallImageConclusion (uniform rectifiability) all match. The scope note [S1:9]{1} records the same alignment.
