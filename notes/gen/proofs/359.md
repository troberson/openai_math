# 359 — Negative Kähler curvature without bounded holomorphic coordinates

## A pinched Hartogs domain

**Match.** The scope note The formalized result constructs a nonempty contractible complex threefold with a complete Kähler metric whose real sectional curvatures lie between two fixed negative constants. Nevertheless, it has no system of bounded holomorphic coordinates and is not biholomorphic to a bounded domain. The metric is obtained from an explicitly controlled potential. This is the negatively pinched construction; the different companion with only one-sided curvature control is separate. [S1:9] states the existence of a
K\"ahler form with a bounded eigenvalue ratio (pinch condition) on
a complex manifold. The declaration
