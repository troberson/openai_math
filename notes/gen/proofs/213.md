## No infinite critical clusters on quasi-transitive graphs

**Partial (1 of 2).** The scope note The critical-percolation question asks whether an infinite cluster can remain at the threshold. The formalized result proves that, at their respective critical probabilities, nearest-neighbor bond and site percolation on $`\mathbb Z^3`$ almost surely have no infinite cluster. Equivalently, almost surely every vertex belongs to a finite cluster in each model. [S1:10] claims the
$`\mathbb{Z}^3`$ result and The formalization proves absence of an infinite cluster at criticality for Bernoulli bond percolation on every infinite connected locally finite quasi-transitive graph with critical probability $`p_c<1`$. At $`p=p_c`$, the probability that any infinite cluster exists is zero. The graph model permits bond multiplicities. [S1:12] claims the quasi-transitive
generalization. The `CriticalZ3` comparator is proven: the declaration

    (∀ᵐ ω ∂siteLaw siteCritical, ∀ x : Vertex, (siteCluster ω x).Finite) :=
  ⟨bond_critical_no_infinite, site_critical_no_infinite⟩
```
