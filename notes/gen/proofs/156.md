# 156 — Borsuk's conjecture fails in dimension nine

## Borsuk's conjecture fails in dimension nine

**Match.** The single comparator target is proven as a `main_results` entry.

The preprint's main theorem [[2]] claims the compact set X = {uuᵀ : u ∈ ℝ⁴, ‖u‖=1} in the trace-one symmetric 4×4 matrices, with the Frobenius metric, has diameter √2 and cannot be covered by ten subsets of strictly smaller diameter. The scope note [S1:9] records the same: rank-one projectors onto lines in ℝ⁴, nine-dimensional affine space, diameter √2, no ten-set cover.

The Lean declaration `main_theorem` [S2:49–56] [[1]] is a four-conjunct theorem:

1. `IsCompact projectorSet` — the set is compact.
2. `projectorSet ⊆ traceOneSymmetric` — it lies in the trace-one symmetric matrices.
3. `Metric.diam projectorSet = Real.sqrt 2` — the diameter is exactly √2.
4. `¬ HasTenSmallCover` — no ten-set cover of strictly smaller diameter exists.

The proof assembles three separately proven lemmas (`isCompact_projectorSet`, `projectorSet_subset_traceOneSymmetric`, `diam_projectorSet`) and derives the cover obstruction from `no_admissible_ten` composed with `cover_to_admissible`. The single comparator [S1:15] (`BorsukNine.lean`) is the shared target.
