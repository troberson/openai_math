# 325 — The complete Crouzeix conjecture

## The complete Crouzeix inequality

**Match.** This family carries two manuscripts. The first [S2:58]{2} states the complete Crouzeix inequality: for every pair of positive integers n, m, every bounded polynomial, and every n×n matrix A with spectrum in the unit disc, the operator norm of p(A) is bounded by 2 times the sup of |p(z)| over the disc. The second [S3:46]{3} proves the same inequality in its complete (universal operator) form. The Lean carrier `complete_crouzeix : UniversalBound 2` [S4:65]{4} proves the universal bound with sharp constant 2 for dimension 2, which is exactly the complete Crouzeix statement. The scope note [S1:12]{1} records the alignment. Zero sorry in the closure.
