# 181 — The Erdős–Gallai cycle-decomposition conjecture

## The Erdős–Gallai cycle-decomposition conjecture

**Match.** The single comparator target is proven as a `main_results` entry.

The preprint's main theorem [[2]] states that there is an absolute constant C > 0 such that every finite simple undirected graph G with n vertices has its edges partitioned into at most Cn cycles or single edges. The scope note [S1:9] records the same, noting edgeless and small graphs are included and the optimal C is not determined.

The Lean declaration `erdos_gallai` [S2:207–216] [[1]] proves `MainStatement`: ∃ C > 0, ∀ n, ∀ G on n vertices, an edge-disjoint decomposition into at most Cn cycles and singleton edges. The proof assembles `main_cost_bound` (which gives the base constant and per-graph cost) and `Batch.inductionPhi_bounds` (which bounds the inductive overhead), closing with `nlinarith`. The single comparator [S1:15] (`CycleDecomposition.lean`) is the shared target.
