# 198 — A counterexample to the little finitistic-dimension conjecture

## An algebra of infinite little finitistic dimension

**Match.** The manuscript's headline [S2:9] is a finite-dimensional complex algebra whose finite-dimensional modules have unbounded finite projective dimensions, disproving the little finitistic-dimension conjecture. The Lean carrier `exists_counterexample` [S3:44] states exactly this: a finite-dimensional complex algebra with littleFinitisticDimension = top and, for every m ≥ 1, a finitely generated module of projective dimension at least 2m−2 and still finite. The scope note [S1:9] records the same alignment. Zero sorry in the closure.
