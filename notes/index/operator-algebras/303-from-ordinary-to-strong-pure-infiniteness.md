## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Resolves the ordinary-to-strong pure-infiniteness question of Kirchberg and R{\o}rdam for complex $C^*$-algebras. For exact algebras, proper infiniteness of one fixed finite amplification of every positive element also suffices. Consequently, every separable nuclear algebra with this property absorbs $\mathcal O_\infty$, without unitality or simplicity assumptions.
