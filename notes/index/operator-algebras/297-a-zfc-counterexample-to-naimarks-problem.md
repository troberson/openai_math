## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Gives an alternative to \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26930v1}{Tanaka's ZFC construction} of a unital infinite-dimensional simple complex $C^*$-algebra with a faithful tracial state and exactly one nonzero irreducible representation up to unitary equivalence. Thus the unrestricted compact-operator characterization fails without additional set-theoretic assumptions; the counterexample is nonseparable.
