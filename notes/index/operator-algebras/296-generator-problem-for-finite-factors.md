## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves that every type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor with separable predual is generated by a single operator, equivalently by two self-adjoint operators, resolving the generator problem. More strongly, for every irreducible inclusion $P\subset M$ of such factors, the unitaries $u$ with $M=W^*(P,u)$ form a dense $G_\delta$ subset in the trace $2$-norm topology.
