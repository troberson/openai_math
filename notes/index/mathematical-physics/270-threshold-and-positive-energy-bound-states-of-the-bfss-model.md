## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves that the undeformed relative $\mathrm{SU}(N)$ BFSS model has exactly one normalizable zero-energy state for every finite $N\ge2$, resolving the threshold-bound-state conjecture. For $\mathrm{SU}(2)$, a companion proves infinitely many normalizable positive-energy eigenstates with unbounded energies, contradicting the original BFSS paper\textquotesingle s exclusion of additional bound states at $N=2$.
