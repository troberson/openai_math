## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves the entropy photon-number inequality for beam-splitter mixing of two independent finite-energy bosonic inputs in any finite number of modes: the output\textquotesingle s entropy photon number is at least the transmissivity-weighted average of the inputs\textquotesingle. Arbitrary entanglement within each input is allowed, and product thermal inputs attain equality even when their entropies differ.
