## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Resolves Sogge's local smoothing conjecture for the Euclidean wave equation in three spatial dimensions. The estimate holds throughout the full strict range $2<p<\infty$, with Sobolev regularity above $\max\{0,1-3/p\}$. In particular, the critical $L^3$ estimate holds with every positive Sobolev loss.
