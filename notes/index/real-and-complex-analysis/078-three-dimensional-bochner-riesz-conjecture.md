## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Resolves the three-dimensional Bochner--Riesz conjecture in its strict range: the Bochner--Riesz multipliers of order $\delta$ are bounded on $L^p(\mathbb R^3)$ for every $1\le p\le\infty$ whenever $\delta>\max\{3|1/p-1/2|-1/2,0\}$.
