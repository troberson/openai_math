## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves almost-everywhere convergence $e^{it\Delta}f\to f$ as $t\downarrow0$ for every $f\in H^{n/(2(n+1))}(\mathbb R^n)$ and every dimension $n\ge2$. This attains the sharp Sobolev equality case of Carleson's Schr\"odinger convergence problem, including the planar endpoint $H^{1/3}$.
