## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

For every fixed $q\in(0,1)$, constructs compact subsets of $[0,1]$ with measure arbitrarily close to one containing no translated and nontrivially dilated copy of $\{q^n:n\ge1\}$, with dilations of either sign. This resolves the geometric-progression case of the Erd\H{o}s similarity conjecture for every ratio.
