## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Resolves the Kakeya maximal conjecture in three dimensions and the Hausdorff-dimension conjecture in four. In three dimensions, the radius-$\delta$ tube maximal operator maps $L^3(\mathbb R^3)$ to $L^3(S^2)$ with norm $O_\varepsilon(\delta^{-\varepsilon})$ for every $\varepsilon>0$. In four dimensions, every set containing a unit segment in every direction has Hausdorff dimension four.
