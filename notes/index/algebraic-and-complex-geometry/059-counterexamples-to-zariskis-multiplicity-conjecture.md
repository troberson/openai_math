## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Disproves Zariski's multiplicity conjecture by constructing reduced holomorphic hypersurface germs that are ambiently homeomorphic but have different multiplicities. The examples include hypersurfaces in $\mathbb C^4$ with isolated critical points and multiplicities four and five.
