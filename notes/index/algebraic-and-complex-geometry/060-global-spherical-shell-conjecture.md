## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Every connected minimal compact complex surface of class VII with $b_2>0$ contains a global spherical shell, proving the positive-$b_2$ Global Spherical Shell conjecture. Such a shell is a holomorphically embedded neighborhood of the standard three-sphere in $\mathbb C^2\setminus\{0\}$ whose complement is connected.
