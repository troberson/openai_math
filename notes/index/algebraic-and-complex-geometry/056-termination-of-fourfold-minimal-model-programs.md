## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves termination of every existing generalized log canonical flip sequence on globally Weil $\mathbb Q$-factorial compact K\"ahler fourfolds, with rational boundary, fixed rational analytically nef b-data, and projective small flip diagrams with the prescribed ample signs. Also proves termination of arbitrary permitted minimal model programs for projective log canonical fourfolds with rational boundary in characteristic zero.
