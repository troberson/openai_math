## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

If $X$ is a smooth connected complex projective variety and $-K_X$ admits a smooth Hermitian metric with nonnegative curvature, then $H^0(X,-mK_X)\ne0$ for some $m>0$. Thus smooth semipositivity forces a nonzero section of a positive tensor power of the anticanonical bundle in every dimension.
