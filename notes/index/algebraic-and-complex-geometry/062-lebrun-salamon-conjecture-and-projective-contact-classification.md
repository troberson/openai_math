## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves the LeBrun--Salamon conjecture: every closed connected positive quaternionic-K\"ahler manifold of real dimension at least eight is homothetic to a compact symmetric Wolf space. It also proves contact-Fano homogeneity and classifies smooth connected complex projective contact manifolds of complex dimension at least three: those with $b_2=1$ are adjoint varieties with their canonical contact structures, while those with $b_2\ge2$ have underlying manifold $\mathbb P(T^*Z)$ for a smooth projective variety $Z$.
