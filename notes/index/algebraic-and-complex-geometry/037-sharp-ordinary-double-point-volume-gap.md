## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves the ordinary-double-point volume-gap conjecture: every singular complex algebraic klt germ of dimension $n\ge2$, with zero boundary, has normalized volume at most $2(n-1)^n$. Equality holds precisely for an analytic ordinary double point.
