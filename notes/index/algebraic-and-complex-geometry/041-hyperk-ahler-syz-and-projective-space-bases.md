## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves the strong hyperk\"ahler SYZ conjecture: every holomorphic line bundle with nonzero nef isotropic first Chern class on a compact irreducible holomorphic symplectic K\"ahler manifold is semiample. It also proves that every projective Lagrangian fibration with normal projective base has projective space as its base, in every dimension and deformation type.
