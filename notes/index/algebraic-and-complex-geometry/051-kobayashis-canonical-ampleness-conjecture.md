## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Every compact connected K\"ahler manifold of positive complex dimension with no nonconstant entire curve has ample canonical bundle and is therefore projective. This proves Kobayashi's canonical-ampleness conjecture in the smooth compact K\"ahler setting.
