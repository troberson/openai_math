## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves log abundance for projective log canonical threefold pairs over algebraically closed fields of characteristic $p>3$ when the effective boundary is rational and the $\mathbb Q$-Cartier adjoint is nef of numerical dimension one. The adjoint is semiample, without requiring the original variety to be terminal or $\mathbb Q$-factorial.
