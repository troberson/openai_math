## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves topological right-triviality for every holomorphic one-parameter family of isolated hypersurface singularities in $\mathbb C^3$ with constant Milnor number, resolving the surface case of the $\mu$-constant problem. After shrinking the parameter disk and representatives, ambient homeomorphisms vary jointly continuously, fix the origin section and preserve the defining functions.
