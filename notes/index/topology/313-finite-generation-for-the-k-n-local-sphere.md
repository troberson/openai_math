## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Answers the degreewise finiteness question of Hovey and Hovey--Strickland: every homotopy group of the $K(n)$-local sphere is a finitely generated $\mathbb Z_p$-module, for every prime, positive height and integer degree. The same conclusion holds after $K(n)$-localizing any finite $p$-local spectrum.
