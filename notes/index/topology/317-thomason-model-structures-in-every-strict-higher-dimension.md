## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Resolves the Ara--Maltsiniotis conjecture: for every $n\ge1$ and $n=\omega$, small strict globular $n$-categories admit proper combinatorial Thomason model structures Quillen equivalent to simplicial sets. Thus strict higher categories model the homotopy theory of spaces in every stated dimension.
