## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Disproves strong chromatic splitting at height three for primes $p\ge5$, and weak splitting for the derived $p$-completed sphere at heights $p$ ($p\ge5$) and $p+1$ ($p\ge7$). Nevertheless, for $n\ge1$ and $p>n+1$, the overlap $L_{n-1}L_{K(n)}S_p^\wedge$ admits a $2^n$-stage filtration by the predicted localized-sphere pieces. At height three and prime three, even finite assembly from such pieces fails in the category of $E(2)$-local modules over the derived completed sphere.
