## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves that the rational Grothendieck--Teichm\"uller Lie algebra, with the Ihara bracket, is freely generated by one element in each odd weight $3,5,7,\ldots$, resolving the Deligne--Drinfeld conjecture.
