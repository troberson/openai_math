## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Resolves the Erd\H{o}s--Pomerance joint Dickman conjecture: the logarithmic sizes of the largest prime factors of $n$ and $n+1$ are asymptotically independent in ordinary natural density. In particular, the integers satisfying $P^+(n)<P^+(n+1)$ have density $1/2$.
