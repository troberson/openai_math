## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

For every fixed $C>0$, a positive proportion of consecutive prime gaps exceed $C\log p_n$, throughout every sufficiently large initial segment of the primes. The proportion may depend on $C$. Consequently, the indices where $p_n/n$ increases have positive lower density, answering Erd\H{o}s and Prachar.
