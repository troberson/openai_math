## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves the infinitude assertion in Artin's primitive root conjecture for every integer $a$ that is neither $-1$ nor a square. For each such base, at least $c_a x/(\log x)^2$ primes in every sufficiently large interval $(x,2x)$ have primitive root $a$, with $c_a>0$.
