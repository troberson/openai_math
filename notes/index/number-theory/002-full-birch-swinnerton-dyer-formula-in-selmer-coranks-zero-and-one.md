## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves the full Birch--Swinnerton-Dyer leading-term formula for every elliptic curve over $\mathbb Q$ whose full $q$-power Selmer group has corank zero or one for some prime $q$, including finiteness of the Tate--Shafarevich group. With result 006, this gives full BSD for a density-one set of quadratic twists of every elliptic curve over $\mathbb Q$.
