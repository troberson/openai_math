## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves that rational points on every smooth proper geometrically connected curve of genus at least two over a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_p$ correspond bijectively to conjugacy classes of sections of its full arithmetic \'etale fundamental group. It also proves Grothendieck's section conjecture over $\mathbb Q$ for the modular curves $X_0(N)$ and $X_1(N)$ of genus at least two.
