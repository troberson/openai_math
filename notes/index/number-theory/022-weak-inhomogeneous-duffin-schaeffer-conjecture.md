## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves that for every real shift $\gamma$ and finite-valued $\psi:\mathbb N\to[0,\infty)$, divergence of $\sum_q\phi(q)\psi(q)/q$ implies $\|qx-\gamma\|<\psi(q)$ for infinitely many $q$, for almost every $x$. Here $\phi$ is Euler's totient and the norm is distance to the nearest integer. Numerators are unrestricted; no monotonicity or Diophantine condition on $\gamma$ is needed.
