## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

For the square-lattice nearest-neighbor cosine XY model, proves critical axis correlations $C_{\beta_c}(r)\sim Ar^{-1/4}(\log r)^{1/8}$ and the Berezinskii--Kosterlitz--Thouless essential singularity $\sqrt{\beta_c-\beta}\log\xi(\beta)\to B$, with $A,B>0$ after the free-box thermodynamic limit. For finite square-symmetric interactions containing nearest neighbors, discrete Gaussian heights converge to Gaussian fields throughout the rough phase, including its threshold, along geometric torus sizes. Critical center-magnetization and spin-field conclusions retain their stated height, renormalization, and field-input assumptions.
