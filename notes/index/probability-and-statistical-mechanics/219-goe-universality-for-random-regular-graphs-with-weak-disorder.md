## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

For every fixed degree $d\ge3$, the bulk adjacency-eigenvalue point process of a uniform simple random $d$-regular graph converges to the Gaussian orthogonal ensemble law, including for cubic graphs. The same fixed-energy universality persists under sufficiently weak fixed iid uniform diagonal disorder, throughout compact bands strictly inside the clean spectral edges.
