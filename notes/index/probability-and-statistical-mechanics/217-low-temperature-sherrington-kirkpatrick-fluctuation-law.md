## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

For every fixed inverse temperature $\beta>1$, determines the fluctuation scale and limiting law of the zero-field Gaussian Sherrington--Kirkpatrick log partition function. Its variance is asymptotic to $c_\beta n^{1/3}$, with $c_\beta>0$, confirming the predicted $n^{1/6}$ standard-deviation scale. Exact centering and standardization give full-sequence convergence to a uniquely characterized nondegenerate law.
