## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves chordal $\mathrm{SLE}_\kappa$ limits for critical square-lattice random-cluster interfaces for $0<q\le4$, with $\kappa=4\pi/\arccos(-\sqrt q/2)$: bounded Jordan domains are allowed for $q\ge1$, and smooth Jordan domains for $q<1$, under the stated marked-boundary approximations. For $1\le q\le4$, complete nested plane loops converge to $\mathrm{CLE}_\kappa$.
