Those hits make it a subject. The prose and bibliography columns are listed as citation evidence: a body paragraph that mentions a name says the manuscript cites it, not that the result is about it. A name that never reaches a title layer has no page — it is a row in [`../names.tsv`](../names.tsv) with `role = citation`.

Prose counts: 64 capitalized occurrences, 58 of them mid-sentence, 12 lowercase. The mid-sentence count is what separates a surname from an English word; the rule and its measurements live in [`notes/names.md`](../../names.md).
