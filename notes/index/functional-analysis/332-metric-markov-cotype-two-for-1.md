## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves that real $\ell_1$ has metric Markov cotype two, answering Mendel and Naor's question. Consequently, every Lipschitz map from an arbitrary subset of a real Hilbert space into $\ell_1$ extends to the whole space with a universal multiplicative loss in its Lipschitz constant, resolving Ball's extension problem for this target.
