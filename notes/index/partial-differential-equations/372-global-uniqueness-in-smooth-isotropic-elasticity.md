## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves that full static boundary displacement-to-traction data determine both smooth real Lam\'e moduli on every bounded connected smooth domain in $\mathbb R^3$, provided $\mu>0$ and $3\lambda+2\mu>0$ on the closure. Neither analyticity, proximity to constant coefficients nor prior knowledge near the boundary is required.
