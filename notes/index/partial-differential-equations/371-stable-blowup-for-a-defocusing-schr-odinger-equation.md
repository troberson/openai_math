## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

For a sufficiently large odd nonlinearity power, constructs a nonempty open set of initial data in $H^k(\mathbb T^{12})$, with $k>8$, whose solutions of the scalar defocusing nonlinear Schr\"odinger equation blow up in finite time. Thus finite-time blowup is stable under Sobolev perturbations in this supercritical regime.
