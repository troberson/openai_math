## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves uniform interior $C^{1,\alpha_d}$ regularity for bounded infinity-harmonic functions in every dimension $d\ge3$, for a positive exponent depending only on dimension. The gradient's supremum norm and H\"older seminorm on the half unit ball are bounded by a dimension-dependent constant times the oscillation on the unit ball. The exponent is not explicit.
