## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Resolves the three-dimensional Ball--Evans approximation problem: every $W^{1,p}$ homeomorphism between arbitrary bounded domains in $\mathbb R^3$, for $1\le p<\infty$, is a strong $W^{1,p}$ limit of smooth diffeomorphisms onto the same target.
