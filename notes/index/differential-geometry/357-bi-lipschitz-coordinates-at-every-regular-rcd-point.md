## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves that every regular point of a noncollapsed $\operatorname{RCD}(K,n)$ space, for $K\in\mathbb R$ and integer $n\ge2$, has an open neighborhood bi-Lipschitz to an open subset of $\mathbb R^n$. Regularity requires all pointed tangents to be Euclidean, the reference measure is exactly $\mathcal H^n$, and the chart compares ambient distances with a point-dependent finite constant.
