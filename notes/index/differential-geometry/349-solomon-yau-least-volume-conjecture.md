## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves the Solomon--Yau least-volume conjecture for minimal hypersurfaces of round spheres. For every $m\ge2$, a closed connected minimal immersion into the unit sphere $S^{m+1}$ with non-totally-geodesic image has volume at least that of the smallest minimal Clifford product, counting covering multiplicity.
