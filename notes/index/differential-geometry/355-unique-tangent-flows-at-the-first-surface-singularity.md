## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves the first-singular-time case of tangent-flow uniqueness for smooth compact connected embedded surfaces without boundary in $\mathbb R^3$. At every singular point, all fixed-center backward tangent flows agree as area measures at every negative time in the original ambient coordinates, without mean-convexity or a prescribed tangent model.
