## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Proves that every finite system of equations over an arbitrary group whose exponent-sum matrix has full row rank over $\mathbb Q$ has a simultaneous solution in an overgroup, resolving Howie's conjecture. The coefficient group embeds in the presented quotient. A companion proves Kervaire's conjecture: adjoining one generator and one relation cannot trivialize a nontrivial group.
