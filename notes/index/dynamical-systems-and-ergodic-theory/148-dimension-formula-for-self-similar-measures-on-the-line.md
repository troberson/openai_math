## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

For every self-similar measure on the line generated by finitely many contracting similarities, proves $\dim_{\mathrm H}\mu=\min\{1,h_{\mathrm{RW}}/\chi\}$, where $h_{\mathrm{RW}}$ is the entropy rate of random composed maps and $\chi$ the average logarithmic contraction. This resolves the entropy-rate dimension conjecture without a separation assumption, allowing exact overlaps and unequal contraction ratios.
