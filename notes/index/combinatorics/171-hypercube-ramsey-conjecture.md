## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

Resolves the Burr--Erd\H{o}s hypercube Ramsey conjecture: the two-color Ramsey number of the $n$-dimensional cube is $\Theta(2^n)$. Thus every red-blue coloring of a complete graph on a universal constant times the cube's number of vertices contains a monochromatic copy of the cube.
