## Abstract (`overview.tex`, verbatim)

For every fixed $d\ge3$, any $n\ge2$ distinct points in $\mathbb R^d$ determine at least $c_dn^{2/d}$ distinct distances, with $c_d>0$ depending only on dimension. This matches the integer-grid order and resolves the higher-dimensional Erd\H{o}s distinct-distances conjecture with a constant-factor bound.
