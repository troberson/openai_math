# Findings — the 372 families by claim shape, and what the evidence layers show

## The rules behind the counts

`notes/catalog.tsv` [S1] is 372 rows over 22 columns, generated by `notes/scripts/build_catalog.py`
[S2]; every ledger count below is quoted from a fresh run of that report, and the report is the test.
Record-level counts are cited to the record that measures them, and a record's count is quoted with
the rule that yields it. Two scope labels apply throughout: the claim-shape tags run over the
`overview.tex` [S3] abstracts, not over the manuscripts; and nothing here was built — no `lake`, no
`lean`, no comparator — so no count below says a file elaborates.

## The claim-shape division

### Tier is an evidence partition, not a difficulty ranking

**T3 136 · T2 106 · T1 0 · T0 130.** The rule is the evidence a family ships, read from
`lean/formalization.yaml` [S4] and `lean/docs/`: T3 names at least one of the family's comparator
configs in `main_results`; T2 has configs, none named; T1 has a scope note and no configs; T0 has no
scope note. T1 is empty because all 242 scope notes link at least one config [S5], so the partition
is really three tiers, and the 130 T0 families are the 130 families the release ships no Lean
artefact for at all.

### The shape tags are multi-label, so a tag is a floor

Over the 372 overview abstracts: **uniform 161 · resolution 144 · bound 139 · counterexample 44 ·
partial 42 · classification 34 · density 20 · conditional 5 · undecidability 4**. **66 families
match ≥3 shapes and 57 match none**, so the tags are not a census of result types and no family is
classified by its first keyword hit.

### Two columns sit outside the taxonomy on purpose

`resolves_named` flags **248** families (T3 101 · T2 61 · T1 0 · T0 86): the title or abstract names a
conjecture, question, or problem and uses a resolution verb. `counterex_broad` flags **74** (T3 35 ·
T2 18 · T1 0 · T0 21) over **120** manuscripts, by phrase rather than stem. Against the `resolution`
tag the union is **253**, the intersection **139**, the tag-only set **5** (`003 010 082 103 261`),
and the column-only set **109**. Against the `counterexample` tag the tag-only set is **0** and the
flag-only set is **30**. Neither rule is a shape entry: widening `resolution` from 144 to 248 and
`counterexample` from 44 to 74 moves families out of the bound, classification, density,
undecidability and resolution buckets that `notes/scripts/triage_t0.py` assigns by first match, which
is a taxonomy change disguised as a tag tweak [S6][S7].

### Reading corrected each of the three soft tags

**Conditional: 9 flags, 5 genuine.** "Five families are genuinely conditional" [S8:7] — 014, 125,
240, 244, 282. The five dropped flags are a word inside "unconditionally", an adjective on a noun
("conditional laws"), the conditional-expectation operator, and "without assuming", where the absence
of the assumption is the theorem.

**Resolution: the wider rule adds 94, and all 94 are resolutions in kind.** The shipped rule tags
144; the candidate rule (target noun and resolve verb over the abstract) yields 233, and every one of
the 94 abstract-only flags was read [S6]. Nothing was dropped; 3 need qualifiers.

**Counterexample: 74 flagged, 44 tagged, 30 flag-only, all 30 genuine.** The phrase rule excludes
348 ("answers … nonnegatively"), 339 ("strictly negative sectional curvature") and 355 ("at every
negative time"), and catches 004 — a negative resolution of Hilbert's tenth over `Q` with no
counterexample object exhibited [S7].

### The tags describe the blurb, not the manuscript

**No manuscript abstract is the overview abstract: 0 identical, 0 abridged, 719 divergent** [S9][S10].
The median family best match is **0.236**, and **283 of 372 families have at least one shape
disagreement between the two texts; 89 have none** [S9:120]. The disagreement is directional: the
overview over-flags `resolution` — **144** families in the overview against **127** families and
**153** manuscripts in the manuscripts themselves [S9]. For a multi-manuscript family a low match
ratio is expected, since the overview is a synthesis of several manuscripts; the unit is the family
for the 283 and the manuscript for the 153.

### What the 130 T0 families look like once read

The 130 T0 families carry **244** manuscripts, and for all of them "the abstract in `overview.tex` is
the whole body of evidence the release ships for them" [S11:559-560] — no scope note, no comparator
challenge, no `main_results` entry, no reasoning trace. First-match buckets over those 130 families
and 244 manuscripts, first match winning so the buckets are a triage ordering and not a taxonomy:
counterexample **16/29**, resolution **46/85**, bound **27/54**, classification **8/20**, uniform
**12/17**, density/partial/undecidability **2/5**, no shape tag at all **19/34** [S11]. `resolves_named`
flags **86 of 130** and `counterex_broad` **21 of 130**. The resolution bucket is the largest
claim-to-evidence gap in the ledger: 46 families claiming named-target resolutions with no Lean
artefact behind them.
Absence of formalization is not a weakness signal — 547 of 719 manuscripts are unformalized [S11].
Within T0, **35** families ship exactly two manuscripts; **27** of them are the second-pass reading
population, carrying **54** manuscripts, and "no coverage signal" holds for **26** of the 27 and fails
for 268, which carries `verification = 2` [S12].

## What the evidence layers show

Coverage is four layers, not one percentage: **172** of 719 manuscripts formalized, **242** scope
notes, **416** comparator challenges, **200** `main_results` entries over **189** unique configs
reaching **136** families [S2]. The `main_results` set and the formalized set no longer agree at
family level: **135** families have a formalized manuscript and **136** have a `main_results` entry;
the difference is **1** family (`342`) [S13]. The cause is a manuscript the release lists twice
under one title: the yaml `sources:` list carries **173** paths, **1** of them not listed by any
family (`Taming-implies-compatibility-on-four-manifolds-September-23-2026`) [S2], while family 342's
own href is the October-6-2026 directory — its config has a declaration entry, its listed
manuscript has no `sources:` record. That one path is the whole 173-vs-172 gap between the yaml
list and the ledger's `paps_form` sum.

**A comparator file is a challenge stub, not a proof.** The 416 `lean/ComparatorChallenges/*.lean`
files carry **539** `sorry` occurrences, 415 of them at least one; "zero of the 122,458 files under
`lean/OAI/**/*.lean` contain `sorry`" [S8:213-216]. A comparator file states the target the release set
itself; the proved version, where it exists, lives under `lean/OAI/`.

**What the release says about its own formalization is a source-level statement.**
`lean/formalization.yaml` [S4:970] carries `scope: "Partial progress."`, [S4:1775] carries
`- method: agent`, and [S4:1778] carries `status: unchecked`. Zero `sorry` under `lean/OAI/` is not a
comparator pass and does not mean the library elaborates.

**Verification bundles are arithmetic, not proofs.** "A verification bundle is a
**finite-arithmetic certificate**, not a proof check" [S14:11-12]. **30** bundles over **22**
families; **4** ship `INPUTS.md`, **2** ship `COVERAGE.md` (both family 348). **12** of the 30 ship a
README naming the command and its success criteria; **18** ship no README [S14].

**The remaining layers are small.** **10** reasoning traces, all attributed to families by `README.md`
[S15], 0 unattributed, shipping in 11 files (the Mézard–Parisi trace ships as both `.pdf` and `.tex`).
**21** of 372 families have a summary page, covering **57** manuscripts [S2].

**The counterexample families are the best-evidenced and the thinnest-evidenced at once.** The
reading population is the counterexample tag intersected with tier ≠ T0: **28** families (19 T3,
9 T2). All 28 have a scope note; **19** have at least one `main_results` entry and **9** have none,
and all 9 of those are T2 [S16]. Meanwhile the object-level T0 counterexample check covers **21**
families over **34** manuscripts, and all 21 have no scope note, no challenge, no `main_results` entry
and no trace [S11:551-556].

**Scope notes bound the claims in prose, and rarely in vocabulary.** "121 of 242 sections state at
least one exclusion, carrying 143 exclusion statements; 121 state none" [S5:27-28]. A phrase
search finds far less than reading does: the 5-phrase narrow list hits **5** of 242 and the 24-phrase
broad list hits **44** [S17]. A clause saying an assumption is *not imposed* widens the formalized
claim rather than bounding it, so the exclusion count is a reading result, not a keyword result.

## What the manuscript-source layers add

**Proof count is document length, not density.** "No ledger column. Proof density is (a) a length
proxy at the tier level, (b) flat at 0.97 proofs per statement, and (c) silent on every claim shape"
[S18:95-96]. The medians show why: proofs per manuscript **30.0 / 21.2 / 18.0** across T0 / T2 / T3
while tex KB per manuscript is **168.2 / 118.7 / 89.0**, and proofs per statement is
**0.965 / 0.962 / 0.973** [S19]. The direction is opposite the naive reading — T0, the
least-formalized tier, has the most proofs per manuscript because its manuscripts are the largest.
The one shape contrast that clears the bar is inverse: `counterex_broad` at AUC **0.356**,
p **1.22e-04** on proofs per manuscript [S19].

**Named results are named in the manuscript far less often than in the blurb.** Over the 719 resolved
main files: **2,410** headline environment begins, **1,609** of them titled, **271** families with at
least one titled headline and **101** with none, and **17** titles that name a target conjecture
[S20].

**Citation overlap is discipline-local, so it cannot rank consequence.** "At the threshold where
sharing is a research signal, it is not: 6 of 419 pairs at ≥5 shared titles cross a discipline
boundary and 0 of 197 at ≥10" [S21:8-9]. The method is blind to **225** of 719 manuscripts, which
carry an inline `thebibliography` and no `.bib` file [S22].

**Intra-release citation edges exist and carry no direction.** **393** release-labelled bibliography
entries resolve to **391** manuscript→manuscript edges over **156** of 372 families, **104** crossing
a family boundary [S13]. "The 104 cross-family citation edges are **not** typed" [S13:198] — the
release records no builds-on / contrasts-with / refutes relation, so an edge is a citation choice.

**Names split into subjects and citations, and only subjects get a page.** **5,842** measured keys
resolve to **551** subject names and **5,291** citation-only rows over **9,819** family-name edges
[S23]. `cauchy` reaches the prose of **232** families and appears in **0** of the 200 `main_results`
declaration names — a fact about the release, not an extractor gap [S23].

**Topics are curated, not derived.** **92** terms carry **978** assignments over the 372 families,
**0** families are uncovered, and **4** terms have no hit and are kept as measured negatives [S24].
A term is quoted with its per-layer counts: a yield of 24 can be 2 titles and 16 scope notes.

**Multi-claim families pair unevenly.** The reading population is **49 families over 203
manuscripts** [S25:10]. Of the titles the release displays, **109** are claimed by an abstract
clause and **94** by none; **62** sub-finding clause pairs and **47** headline clause pairs were
accepted, and 18 clauses were recorded unmatched [S25].

**The LaTeX surface has no single convention.** **724** `\documentclass` roots over **719** listed
manuscript directories (715 with one, 3 with two, 1 with three), while `preprints/` holds **749**
directories; main-file resolution answers the same file for all 719 under either naming rule, and
**every one of the 719 resolved main files sits under a `build/`
directory** [S26]. Proof environments total **20,341**; statement environments total **21,288** by the
declared rule and **21,203** by the six-name list, the 85 gap being `assumption`, `foundation`,
`auxproposition`, `hypothesis`, `auxlemma` and 10 names at ≤5 [S26].

**The rendered index is link-clean.** **1,033** pages carry **66,107** links with **0** broken [S27].

## The dependency record

The formalization is pinned, patched, and toolchain-normalised: **30** declared library pins, **23**
patches over **160,187** lines carrying **734** `diff --git` hunks, **22** of the 23 patches rewriting
`lean-toolchain`, and **8** distinct source toolchains all normalised to `v4.34.1` [S28]. The patch
volume is **1.3** lines per source file [S28]. Import counts over `lean/OAI/**/*.lean` are OAI
**277,607** and Mathlib **56,416** [S29]; the same rule over `lean/**/*.lean` (122,876 files) yields
278,523 / 56,944, the additions being `lean/OAI.lean`'s module imports and the 416 challenge stubs,
which import Mathlib **528** times and OAI **3** times.

## The prior, labelled as a prior

`notes/conjecture-standing.tsv` [S30] carries **139** rows — `resolves_named` intersected with the
`resolution` tag — and its standing column is "**the assistant's training-data prior, not a literature
survey**" [S31:4]. Surprise tiers read 1:60, 2:64, 3:11, 4:4; `prior_confidence` reads high 42,
medium 84, low 13. Tier 3 is the deflation set: 11 families whose headline the prior already holds as
a theorem. Row **145** is the only row where the prior contradicts the claim — published counterexamples
to the Rokhlin multiple mixing property against an abstract claiming the property — and the record
stops there [S31]. The negative control (reading the literature record for a sample of rows) has not
been run, so the tiers are a prior-based ranking, not a finding about the release.

## The recurring measurement lesson

Six counts in this repo's own beads were quoted before anyone wrote down the rule that yields them,
and each one is now reproducible or recorded as not reproducible: the counterexample figures **74 /
30**, which no stem variant of the quoted vocabulary reproduces and which the written phrase rule does
[S7]; the sub-finding population **51**, which does not reproduce — the recorded rule yields **49**
families over 203 manuscripts, and nine of the ten named densest families sit outside the probe's own
40 [S25:15]; the "14 of 372 family titles in the `.bib` files" figure, which reproduces under a 6-token
lowercased title prefix over 417 of 500 files while its conclusion (that the manuscripts do not cite
each other) does not follow from it [S13]; the preprint root counts **717 / 5**, which the line-start
rule answers as **715 / 4** over the 719 dirs the release lists today — the substring rule over the same
719 reads **714 / 5**, and the rules differ in exactly one directory that ships a documentation macro
mentioning the command [S26]; the counterexample reading set, which is **21** families and not the
**16** the tag implies [S11]; and the comparator-table figure **426**, which is scope-note link
*occurrences* and not configs — the 10-gap against **416** is one config linked twice inside each of
7 scope notes, so a per-family `set()` over that table reports 416 rows and calls the result a row
count [S13]. Quote a count with the rule that yields it, and a count with no rule is a count that will
not survive a re-run.

## Not checked

- **Nothing was built.** No `lake`, no `lean`, no `lake exe cache get`, no comparator. Zero `sorry`
  under `lean/OAI/` is a lexical fact; no statement here says any file elaborates, and the `main_results`
  declaration names were resolved by reading, not by a build.
- **No manuscript proof was read.** Every claim-shape statement is a statement about the
  `overview.tex` abstract, and the 283-family disagreement shows the two texts differ. The 94
  unclaimed display titles are the visible part of that gap.
- **Consequence is unmeasured.** The 391 citation edges are untyped, so no family in this page is
  presented as building on, refuting, or enabling another.
- **The prior is unvalidated.** No row of `notes/conjecture-standing.tsv` was checked against the
  literature; `prior_confidence` is self-reported by the same prior it qualifies.
- **The 130 T0 families are not all read at object level.** 44 families over 134 manuscripts were read
  in the first pass and 21 families over 34 manuscripts in the counterexample check; the remaining 93
  families of that run, over 121 manuscripts, were covered at abstract level in the second pass, and
  the 27-family two-manuscript chain population is read only for the coverage-signal question.
- **The 18 README-less verification bundles** ship no recorded command, so what they check is
  inferred from their file names, not from a stated criterion.
- **Not measured:** proof density per discipline (only per tier and per shape); the 101 families with
  no titled headline (why the manuscript names no headline is a reading question); the 225
  manuscripts invisible to the citation method; the 4 topics with no hit; the 21 of 372 families that
  have a summary page, which is a coverage fraction, not a coverage claim.
