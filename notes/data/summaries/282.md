## undergraduate

A **symmetry** of a physics theory is a transformation — rotate,
shift, rescale — that leaves the laws unchanged. **Scale
symmetry** means the theory looks the same at every size; **conformal
symmetry** is the larger family that also allows local
rescalings of spacetime. A long-standing question in quantum field
theory is when scale symmetry *forces* conformal symmetry. This paper
answers it in four dimensions, under explicit assumptions, for a
well-behaved class of theories: it shows that if the theory has a
physical "scale current" (the conserved quantity that generates
rescalings) and the field spectrum is discrete and controlled, then the
scale symmetry upgrades to **local conformal symmetry** — the
conformal currents satisfy unbroken **local Ward identities**
[S3:50–67]. The careful caveat, spelled out in the abstract:
the conclusion is *local* (about identities the currents obey on the
field algebra), and does not yet integrate to a **global** conformal
action on the whole algebra — that remains a separate, open
question.

## graduate

**Scale-to-conformal enhancement in four dimensions**
[S3:50–67, S2:656].
For a four-dimensional unitary positive-energy quantum field theory with
a Poincar'e- and scale-invariant vacuum, a discrete bounded-below
spectrum of scaling dimensions of finite multiplicity, finite scaling
support for each original field, compatible affiliated realizations of
the fields and their adjoints in a causally commuting bounded local net,
and a physical local dilatation current generating scale transformations
through its local Ward identities, the stress tensor admits a symmetric,
conserved, **traceless** improvement with unchanged translation and
Lorentz charges, and the corresponding conformal currents satisfy
unbroken local Ward identities on the physical field algebra [S3:50–67].
The result is conditional on those hypotheses, and the conclusion is
explicitly **local**: integration to a global conformal action on the
whole bounded net remains a separate question [S3:65–67].

## history

The question of when scale invariance implies conformal invariance has a
long history: in two dimensions the two coincide for rational CFTs, and
in four dimensions the improvement-program for the stress tensor going
back to Friedan (1980) and the algebraic approaches of Longo, M"{o}ller,
and collaborators set up the tools to pass from a dilatation current to
conformal currents. The obstruction in higher dimensions is that a
traceless improvement of the stress tensor need not exist without extra
regularity on the spectrum and on the local net. The manuscript
[S3:50–67] supplies a concrete, checkable set of hypotheses — discrete
bounded-below scaling spectrum of finite multiplicity, finite scaling
support, causally commuting bounded local net, and a physical local
dilatation current — under which the traceless improvement exists with
unchanged spacetime charges and the conformal currents satisfy unbroken
local Ward identities. It stops short of the global statement by design
[S3:65–67].

**Prior (my training data, not this repo).** Scale-to-conformal
enhancement, the Friedan stress-tensor improvement, dilatation and
conformal currents, and local Ward identities in algebraic QFT are
standard in conformal field theory and algebraic quantum field theory.
The specific four-dimensional conditional result — the named hypothesis
set under which the traceless improvement exists and the conformal
currents obey unbroken local Ward identities, with the global action
expressly left open — is the novel contribution. I cannot confirm this
independently from training alone.
