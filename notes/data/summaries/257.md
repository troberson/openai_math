## undergraduate

A group is **quasi-isometric** to a CAT(0) space — a
space where triangles are at least as thin as on a flat plane,
so the geometry is "nonpositively curved" — if the group
"acts geometrically" on it: properly (finite pieces meet only
finitely), cocompactly (a finite chunk tiles the whole space),
and by isometries (distance-preserving moves). Such groups are
called **CAT(0) groups**. It was an open question whether
**every** word-hyperbolic group (a broad class captured by a
"thin triangles" large-scale condition) is CAT(0). The answer
is **no** — the result constructs a word-hyperbolic group
with a finite classifying space that has **no** such action on
any proper complete CAT(0) space, in any dimension
[S3:9, S2:603].
