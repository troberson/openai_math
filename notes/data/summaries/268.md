A companion establishes a gap for odd open chains with endpoint field $`h=3/5`$ and gives boundary-selected infinite-volume states with topological index −1.

## undergraduate

The **Heisenberg model** describes a chain of tiny magnets (spins)
that prefer to point in opposite directions (antiferromagnetic). A
**spectral gap** is the minimum energy you must supply to "excite" the
system from its ground state. The **Haldane gap conjecture** (Haldane,
1983) predicted that the spin-one antiferromagnetic Heisenberg chain —
the simplest version of this model — has a **uniformly positive**
gap: no matter how long the chain is, there is always a minimum energy
to create an excitation. The first manuscript [S3:90–94] proves
this for **periodic** (ring-shaped) chains with an even number of sites.
The second [S4:41–47] proves a related gap for **open**
chains with an odd number of sites, when a small magnetic field is
applied at both endpoints; it also identifies the **topological index**
of the resulting ground states as $-1$, a signature of the edge modes
that Haldane predicted.
