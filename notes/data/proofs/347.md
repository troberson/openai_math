## Three fixed points on the symplectic quadric threefold

**Match.** The scope note [S1:9]{{1}} states the counterexample: a
Hamiltonian diffeomorphism of the complex quadric threefold with
exactly three fixed points, while every smooth function has at least
four critical points, with at least one fixed point degenerate. The
nondegenerate Morse-number construction is excluded.

quadric threefold $Q^3 \subset \mathbb{C}P^4$ explicitly and

of four. The declaration `OAI.ArnoldCounterexample.main` is proven.
