## Bounded recovery for modular spectral averages

**Partial.**

The scope note [S1:9]{{1}} states the formalized result
converts positive modular spectral averages into uniformly
bounded algebra elements, and closes with the explicit exclusion:

> “The absolute bicentralizer conjecture is not included.” [S1:9]{{1}}

The declaration `bounded_recovery` [S2:227-230]{{2}} in
`Recovery.lean` (266 lines, 0 `sorry`)
proves the modular-recovery lemma: given unit vectors in
shrinking spectral bands around a real number and a positive
limiting symmetric average, it produces a subsequence of bounded
algebra elements whose images under the operator stay uniformly
nonzero. The proof is a non-vacuous `:= by` chain.

The manuscript’s headline claim is the absolute bicentralizer
conclusion for type ℵ₁ factors, which the Lean formalization
does not establish. The Lean proves a supporting component of
the modular spectral average machinery, not the full theorem.

The comparator stub `BoundedRecovery.lean` (103 lines, 1 `sorry`)
carries the target statement [S3:96]{{4}}.
