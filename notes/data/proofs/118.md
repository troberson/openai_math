1	block	S1	87	96	\begin{theorem}[Unbounded configuration gap]\label{thm:unbounded-gap}
For every integer $c\ge0$, there is a finite rational bin-packing instance
$I$ and an integer $B$ such that every item size exceeds $1/6$ and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:main-gap}
  \LP(I)=B,
  \qquad \OPT(I)>B+c.
\end{equation}
The equality for the fractional optimum also holds for the configuration
LP whose patterns are subsets of the individual item copies.
\end{theorem}
2	block	S1	106	112	\begin{theorem}[Constant-additive hardness]\label{thm:additive-hardness}
For every fixed integer $c\ge0$, it is NP-hard to distinguish pairs $(I,B)$
such that $I$ packs into $B$ bins from pairs such that $I$ cannot be packed
into $B+c$ bins.  Here $B$ is an integer supplied with the instance,
the item sizes are rational, and every item size is strictly greater
than $1/6$.
\end{theorem}

## the match

The four conjuncts of `main_results` correspond to the preprint's claims:

| Preprint claim | Lean conjunct | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| $\forall c \ge 0$: instance with $\LP(I) = B$, $\OPT(I) > B+c$, sizes $> 1/6$ [S1:87–96] | `(∀ c, ∃ I, ∃ B, I.n = 5*B ∧ (∀ i, 1/6 < size_i < 1) ∧ B+c < opt I ∧ individualLP I = B ∧ typeLP I = B)` [S2:8–11] | **Match** |
| NP-hard to distinguish $B$-bin from $B+c$-bin [S1:106–112] | `(∀ c, PackingGapNPHard c)` [S2:12] | **Match** |
| — (corollary: $P=NP$ iff additive algorithm) | `((∃ c, Nonempty (AbsoluteAdditiveAlgorithm c)) ↔ PEqualsNP)` [S2:13] | **Match** (corollary) |
| Under $P=NP$, optimal algorithm constructed | `(PEqualsNP → ∃ algorithm : AbsoluteAdditiveAlgorithm 0, …)` [S2:14–22] | **Match** (corollary) |

The first conjunct is a **strengthening** of the preprint's theorem:
- The preprint says "there is a finite rational bin-packing instance"
  [S1:88]; the Lean specifies `I.n = 5 * B` [S2:9] — the instance has
  exactly $5B$ items.
- The preprint says $\LP(I) = B$ [S1:91]; the Lean says `individualLP I = B ∧ typeLP I = B` [S2:11] —
  **both** the individual-copy and size-type configuration LPs equal $B$,
  which is the stronger form stated in the scope note.
- The preprint says "every item size exceeds $1/6$" [S1:89]; the Lean
  adds the upper bound `I.size i < 1` [S2:10].

The NP-hardness conjunct [S2:12] matches the preprint's second theorem
verbatim: "for every fixed integer $c \ge 0$, it is NP-hard to
distinguish pairs $(I,B)$ such that $I$ packs into $B$ bins from pairs
such that $I$ cannot be packed into $B+c$ bins."

The two corollary conjuncts (the $P=NP$ equivalence and the optimal
algorithm construction) go beyond the preprint's theorems — they are
consequences the preprint states in its consequences section.
