## Logarithmic independence bound for clique-free graphs

**Match.** The single comparator target is proven as a `main_results` entry.

The preprint's main theorem [[2]] states that for every r ≥ 4, there is c_r > 0 such that every finite simple K_r-free graph G with n vertices and average degree d ≥ 2 satisfies α(G) ≥ c_r·n·log d/d. The scope note [S1:9] records the same, noting the constant depends only on r.

The Lean declaration `logarithmic_independence_bound` [S2:22–27] [[1]] proves the same bound: for r ≥ 4, ∃ c > 0 such that every K_r-free simple graph with average degree ≥ 2 has independence number at least c·n·log(d)/d. The proof assembles `exists_optimizer_triangle_bound` to get a triangle-mass constant B, sets the bound constant to 1/(16·D) where D = 3+3B/2, and proceeds by the cross-mass iteration. The single comparator [S1:15] (`CliqueFreeLog.lean`) is the shared target.
