## Translational tile with no periodic tiling in three dimensions

**Match.** The single comparator target is proven as a `main_results` entry.

The preprint's main theorem [[2]] claims a finite nonempty T ⊂ ℤ³ that tiles ℤ³ by translations with no fully periodic complement (part i), and whose unit-cube thickening T+[0,1]³ tiles ℝ³ up to null sets with no translation-set-invariant complement (part ii).

The Lean declaration `periodic_tiling_counterexample_and_minimality` [S2:23–31] [[1]] is a two-conjunct theorem. The first conjunct is the five-clause counterexample: T is nonempty, T tiles the lattice (∃A, Tiles T A), no lattice complement is fully periodic (∀A, Tiles T A → ¬FullyPeriodic A), the thickening AETiles the space, and no space complement is Euclidean-fully-periodic. The second conjunct is the minimality: 3 is the least dimension d for which a tile without a fully periodic complement exists (IsLeast {d \| HasTileWithoutPeriodicComplement d} 3).

The proof assembles two separately proven lemmas: `exists_three_dimensional_tile` (the counterexample) and `least_lattice_dimension_three` (the minimality). The scope note [S1:9] records both the counterexample and the minimality, excluding the word-rule/Sudoku encodings, activation and stacking constructions, and the full Euclidean proof beyond the final periodicity obstruction. The single comparator [S1:15] (`PeriodicTilingThree.lean`) is the shared target.
