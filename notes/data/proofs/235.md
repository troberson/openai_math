## Random-SAT thresholds, sharp variance and computability

**Not formalized.** The scope note claims [S1:11]{{1}} that
"The formalization proves" the limiting threshold, the variance bounds,
and the computability of the 3-SAT threshold. All four comparators are
`sorry` stubs with no `main_results` entries in `formalization.yaml`.

The manuscript's main theorem [S5:31–43]{{2}} states the
all-$k$ threshold: for every fixed integer $k\ge 3$, there is a
finite positive $\alpha_k$ such that the satisfiability probability
tends to one below and zero above the threshold. The companion papers
prove the variance bounds $\mathrm{Var}(H_n)=\Theta_k(n)$ for $k\ge 4$
and the computability of $\alpha_3$.

The four stubs cover the full scope: `FixedClauseThreshold` [S2:51–52]{{3}}{{4}},
`SATVariance` [S3:54]{{5}}, `SATComputability` [S4:45]{{6}},
and `SATSharpness`. Each carries a single `sorry`. The scope note's
three separate "The formalization proves" clauses are overclaims: no
Lean file under `lean/OAI/` carries any of the stated results, and no
`main_results` entry names any of the four configs.
