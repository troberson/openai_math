## The exponential time hypothesis is false

**Match.** The single comparator target is proven as a `main_results` entry.

The preprint's main theorem [[2]] states that for each fixed k ≥ 2, a deterministic algorithm computes the exact number of satisfying assignments of a k-CNF in (n+m)^{O_k(1)}·2^{O_k(n·log log n/√log n)} bit operations and (n+m)^{O_k(1)} bits of space, with a linear-density clause giving 2^{O_{k,D}(n/√log n)}.

The Lean declaration `exact_count_and_sat` [S2:71–97] [[1]] is a single existential over a primitive machine, two designated tapes, and positive constants C, A, S, d, e. For every width-bounded k-CNF:

- **Count**: the countTape holds the binary encoding of SAT.count(F), and the answerTape and final state carry the satisfiability bit.
- **Time**: the execution time is bounded by `runtimeBound C A d (formulaBitLength F) n`, which encapsulates the subexponential 2^{O_k(n·log log n/√log n)} form.
- **Space**: at every execution prefix, the visited tape space is at most S·(formulaBitLength+1)^e — polynomial in the input size, matching the preprint's (n+m)^{O_k(1)} bits.

The scope note [S1:9–11] records the count, SAT decision, square-root-logarithmic time, all-prefix polynomial space, and the linear-density clause as the selected formalization surface. The single comparator [S1:15] (`SubexponentialSAT.json`) is the shared target.
