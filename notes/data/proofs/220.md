## Directional zero-one laws and ballisticity

**Match (3 of 3).** All three comparators have `main_results` entries
with OAI proofs (0 `sorry`). The scope note makes three claims: the
zero-one law under strict ellipticity [S1:10]{{1}}, ballisticity from
transience [S1:12]{{2}}, and the velocity hemisphere.

The manuscript's zero-one theorem [S3:61–67]{{3}} states that for
$d\ge 3$ and iid strictly elliptic environments, the directional
escape probability is $0$ or $1$. The ballisticity theorem
[S4:50–56]{{4}} states that a.s. directional transience implies
convergence to a deterministic velocity with positive projection.
