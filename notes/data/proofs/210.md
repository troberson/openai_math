## Quadratic stabilization of the canonical Foulkes–Howe map

**Partial.** The manuscript's headline [S2:91] is the sixth-symmetric-power case of Foulkes' conjecture: for every b ≥ 6 and every finite-dimensional complex V, there is a GL(V)-equivariant injection Sym^6(Sym^b V) into Sym^b(Sym^6 V). The Lean carrier `canonical_foulkes_howe_surjective` [S3:15] proves surjectivity of the averaged Foulkes–Howe map in the range a ≥ 2, b ≥ a(a−1). For a=6 this gives b ≥ 30, not the manuscript's full b ≥ 6 range. The scope note [S1:9] records this gap explicitly: "the companion's full range b ≥ 6 is not included." The Lean proves a genuine subcase (b ≥ 30 for the sixth power), zero sorry, but the headline claim (b ≥ 6) is not in the carrier.
