## Planar first-passage geometry and the absence of bigeodesics

**Partial (1 of 2).** The scope note [S1:9]{{1}} claims
differentiability for "independent Gamma edge weights of every positive
shape and rate," with the exponential model as a special case. The
`PlanarFirstPassage` comparator is proven: the declaration
`OAI.PlanarFPP.manuscriptMain` [S2:65–81]{{3}} in
`BoundaryCurve.lean` (81 lines, 0 `sorry`) constructs a time-constant
norm that is differentiable away from the origin, has a $C^1$ unit
sphere, a unique supporting line at every boundary point, and a $C^1$
curve chart on the boundary. The `GammaPassage` comparator
[S3:107–108]{{5}} has no `main_results` entry and its stub is
discharged by `sorry`.

The manuscript's main theorem [S4:21–32]{{4}} states Fréchet
differentiability and strict convexity for the exponential model. The
Lean proof covers the differentiability, $C^1$ boundary, and unique
supporting line; the scope note explicitly excludes strict convexity
[S1:11]{{2}}. The Gamma generalization (the scope note's headline
claim) is absent from the formalization.
