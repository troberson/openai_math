## proof structure

The preprint's proof strategy [S1:161–195] reduces to six steps:

1. Finite slab approximation (finitely many spanning unit vectors $p_i$ with widths $h_i$).
2. Smooth weight: $\exp(-\frac{\epsilon}{2}|x|^2 - \sum_i (\frac{\langle p_i, x\rangle}{h_i})^q)$.
3. Log-concavity as each $h_i$ varies geometrically.
4. Second derivative of $\log$ integral = variance minus expected second derivative.
5. Variance bound in **moment coordinates** (Berman–Berndtsson + Klartag).
6. Tensor calculation: remainder is a sum of explicit squares; two Cauchy–Schwarz close the argument.

## dimension handling

The preprint states $n \ge 1$ uniformly. The Lean separates the cases: `main_of_softVariance` [S2:20209–20211] handles `n = 1` by a dedicated lemma (`main_dimension_one`) and restricts the analytic core to `n ≥ 2` (the `SoftVarianceStatement` quantifies over `2 ≤ n`).

**Consistent.** In 1D an origin-symmetric convex body is an interval $[-a, a]$ and the Wulff combination is $[-a^{1-t}b^t, \ldots]$, making the inequality trivial. The slab-partition machinery requires `Nonempty (Fin n)` and the Berman–Berndtsson moment map, both of which need $n \ge 2$. No mathematical gap.

## support function scope

The preprint writes $h_K(u)$ for unit $u$ in the Wulff body definition. The Lean defines `support K u` for **all** $u$ [S2:23], then restricts to unit vectors inside `wulff` [S2:26]: `∀ u, ‖u‖ = 1 → ⟨x, u⟩ ≤ f u`. The `logCombination` [S2:29–30] computes `support K u` for all $u$, feeds it to `wulff`, which only evaluates on unit vectors. The homogeneity lemma `support_smul` [S2:19104] ensures consistency.

**Consistent.** Standard convention: define broadly, restrict at the application point.
