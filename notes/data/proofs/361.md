## A three-dimensional counterexample to the integer-degree harmonic dimension comparison

**Match.** The manuscript's headline [S2:31] is the counterexample: for every v in (4/9,1) and c in (1,9v/4), there is an integer k0 such that for every k >= k0, there is a complete smooth metric g_k on R^3, Euclidean near the origin, with Ricci >= 0, AVR = v, and the harmonic dimensions violating the comparison inequality.
