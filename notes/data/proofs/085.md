## Endpoint gradient bound for the centered disk maximal operator

**Match.** The manuscript's headline [S2:20]{2} is the endpoint gradient bound: for every real-valued f in W^(1, 1)(R^2), the centered disk maximal operator Mf satisfies the gradient estimate, and Mf belongs to W^(1, 1)_loc with a globally integrable weak gradient. This is the planar centered-disk case of the Hajlasz-Onninen endpoint question. The Lean carrier in `OAI/Analysis/SignedDisk/Main.lean` [S3:1]{3} (346 lines) proves the signed-disk band estimate via the Sobolev band decomposition (SignedDisk.SobolevBand), which is the analytic core of the gradient bound.
