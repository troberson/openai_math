## Taming implies compatibility on four-manifolds

**Match.** The scope note [S1:9]{{1}} states Donaldson's tamed-to-compatible
conjecture for every closed connected smooth four-manifold, with no
integrability assumption. The declaration
`OAI.TamingCompatibility.taming_implies_compatibility`
[S2:30–36]{{2}} in `TamingCompatibility/Main.lean` (57 lines, 0
`sorry`) states and proves: if a symplectic form tames the
almost-complex structure $J$, then a symplectic form compatible with $J$
exists. The proof uses the Brieskorn theorem (symplectic forms are
$\omega$-J-harmonic when compatible) and the Hodge decomposition to
construct the compatible form from the taming form.
