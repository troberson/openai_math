## Critical local smoothing for the three-dimensional wave equation

**Match.** The manuscript's headline [S2:26] is the critical local smoothing estimate at p=3: for every ε > 0 there exists C_ε such that the L³ norm of the wave solution over ℝ³×[1,2] is bounded by C_ε times the L³ norm of the Bessel potential of order ε.
