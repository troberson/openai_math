## Kadison's similarity conjecture

**Not formalized.** The scope note claims [S1:9]{{1}} that
"The formalization proves this for arbitrary Hilbert spaces." Both
comparators are `sorry` stubs with no `main_results` entries in
`formalization.yaml` and no Lean file under `lean/OAI/`.

The manuscript's main theorem [S4:15–25]{{2}} states Kadison's
similarity problem in full generality: every bounded unital homomorphism
from a unital complex $C^*$-algebra into $\mathcal{B}(H)$ is similar to
a $*$-homomorphism, with no separability, nuclearity, or finite
generation condition. The companion result gives a universal
hyperreflexivity constant for all unital von Neumann algebras.

The two stubs cover the scope: `KadisonSimilarity` [S2:115–116]{{3}}{{4}}
carries three theorems (`universalCommutatorTheorem`,
`universalHyperreflexivity`, `similarityTheorem`), each discharged by
`sorry`; `UniformCommutator` [S3:26]{{5}} states the uniform
amplified commutator estimate with a single `sorry`. The scope note's
"The formalization proves" is an overclaim: no declaration is proven.
