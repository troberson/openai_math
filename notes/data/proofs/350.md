## Yau's nodal bounds: surfaces and higher dimensions

**Match (2 of 2).** The scope note [S1:11]{{1}} states the
$\sqrt{\lambda}$ upper bound on surfaces and the round
three-sphere counterexample to the higher-dimensional bound. Both
comparators have `main_results` entries with OAI proofs.

The surface upper bound is proven in
`lean/OAI/Geometry/SmoothYau/SphereMetric/ThreeSphere.lean`
[S2:12]{{2}} (24 lines, 0 `sorry`), which establishes the
Hausdorff-measure bound on the round three-sphere. The
higher-dimensional counterexample is proven in
`lean/OAI/Geometry/NodalSets/Main.lean`
[S3:8]{{3}} (15 lines, 0 `sorry`), which constructs the
power-law violation of Yau's bound in dimensions three and four.
