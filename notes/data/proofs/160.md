## Superexponential van der Waerden numbers

**Match.** The single comparator target is proven as a `main_results` entry.

The preprint's main theorem [[2]] claims an absolute K₀ such that W_r(k) > k^{c·k·⌊log₂r⌋} for all k ≥ K₀, r ≥ 2, with c = 10⁻⁵.

The Lean declaration `uniform_lower_bound` [S2:12–15] [[1]] states the same bound with the real base-2 logarithm: ∃ K, ∀ k ≥ K, ∀ r ≥ 2, k^{(1/100000)·k·log₂(r)} < W(r,k). The companion `uniform_lower_bound_floor` [S2:18–26] restates this with the floor ⌊log₂r⌋₊, matching the preprint's ⌊log₂r⌋ exactly. The two are equivalent via `natLog_two_eq_floor_log` (line 25). The proof assembles `uniform_lower_of_cyclicLowerBound` and `exists_cyclic_coloring`.

The scope note [S1:9] records the floor form and excludes the sharper intermediate estimates used in the paper. The single comparator [S1:15] (`QuantitativeVanDerWaerden.lean`) is the shared target.
