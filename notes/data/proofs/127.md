## proof structure

The proof has three layers:

1. **`polynomialThreshold_averageSensitivity_le`** [S2:111–119] — the
   general case, including cube zeros. Reduces to the nonzero case via
   `exists_nonzero_polynomial_same_threshold`, which constructs a nearby
   polynomial $q$ with the same threshold function but no zeros.

2. **`polynomialThreshold_averageSensitivity_le_of_nonzero`** [S2:95]
   — the nonzero case. Constructs the weighted projection family from
   $|p(x)|$, verifies the trace, tridiagonality, and block-vanishing
   properties, and delegates to the cube-grading lemma.

3. **`sensitivity_bound_of_cube_grading`** [S2:49] — the finite-matrix
   bound assuming an orthogonal projection family with binomial
   multiplicities, coordinate tridiagonality, and low-block vanishing.
   The proof uses the edge-recovery identity and the anticommutator
   energy estimate to get the final $8d\sqrt{n}$ bound.

The constant 8 arises from the scalar bridge `normalized_edge_bound`
[S2:24–33]: $C \le 2T$ and $T \le 4d\sqrt{n} \cdot 2^n$ give
$C/2^n \le 8d\sqrt{n}$.
