## Optimal logarithmic mixing of the Thorp shuffle

**Partial (3 of 13).** The scope note [S1:10]{{1}} covers the
first-reciprocal structure, the routing of a marked card, the weighted
compatibility of the sweep structure, and the binary-sweep contraction.
Three comparators have `main_results` entries with OAI proofs:
`ThorpRouting` (`OAI.thorp_routing.main`, in `ThorpRouting/Main.lean`,
12 lines, 0 `sorry`), `ThorpWeightedCompatibility`
(`OAI.ThorpWeightedCompatibility.weightedCompatibility_main`, in
`ThorpCompatibility/Main.lean`, 59 lines, 0 `sorry`), and `BinarySweep`
(`OAI.BinarySweep.binary_sweep_contraction_and_mixing`, in
`BinarySweep/Main.lean`, 141 lines, 0 `sorry`). The remaining ten
comparators have no `main_results` entries.

The manuscript's main theorem [S2:33–42]{{2}} states that the
mixing time of the Thorp shuffle on $\binom{N}{2}$ cards satisfies
$\log t_{\mathrm{mix}} / (N\log 2) \to 1/(2\log 2)$. The Lean proofs
cover the routing, compatibility, and binary-sweep structure; the
logarithmic mixing rate and its optimal constant are not formalized.
