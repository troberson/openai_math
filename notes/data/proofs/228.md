## Continuum phase transitions for radial pair potentials

**Partial (2 of 3).** The scope note makes three claims: the
temperature singularity over a density interval [S1:10]{{1}}, the
fixed-density radial transition [S1:12]{{2}}, and a common radial
transition over a density interval. Two comparators have `main_results`
entries with OAI proofs: `ContinuumTransition`
(`OAI.ContinuumTemperature.main_theorem`, in
`ContinuumTransition/Main.lean`, 66 lines, 0 `sorry`) and
`RadialTransition` (`OAI.RadialTransition.main`, in
`RadialTransition/Main.lean`, 105 lines, 0 `sorry`). The
`RadialDensityInterval` comparator has no `main_results` entry; its
stub is `sorry`.

The manuscript's main theorem [S4:44–50]{{3}} states the existence of
a radial potential with $o(r^{-3})$ tail and a free-energy derivative
jump at $\beta_c\in(1/2,3/2)$ for every density in a nonempty open
interval. The Lean proofs cover the two headline claims; the
common-density-interval variant is not formalized.
