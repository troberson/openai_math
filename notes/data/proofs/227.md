## Critical SK dynamics and the temperature transition

**Partial (2 of 5).** The scope note makes five claims over the
high-temperature spectral gap [S1:12]{{1}}, ratio cutoff
[S1:14]{{2}}, critical mixing bounds, covariance/Rayleigh estimates,
and stretched-exponential barriers. Two comparators have `main_results`
entries with OAI proofs: `CriticalSKMixing`
(`OAI.CriticalSK.critical_mixing_bounds`, in `CriticalMixing/Main.lean`,
51 lines, 0 `sorry`) and `CriticalSK`
(`OAI.CriticalSK.covariance_and_linear_tests`, in
`CriticalSK/Rayleigh.lean`, 627 lines, 0 `sorry`). The three
comparators `SKHighTemperature`, `SKRatio`, and `SKBarriers` have no
`main_results` entries; their stubs are `sorry`.

The manuscript's spectral-gap theorem [S4:33–39]{{3}} states the
Poincaré inequality $\mathrm{Var}_{\mu_0}(f)\le C_\beta
\mathcal{D}_0(f)$ with probability tending to one. The Lean proof
covers the critical mixing bounds and the covariance/Rayleigh
estimates; the high-temperature spectral gap, the ratio cutoff, and
the stretched-exponential barrier results are not formalized.
