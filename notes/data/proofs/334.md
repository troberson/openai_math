## A smooth metric with no local isometric immersion

**Match.** The scope note [S1:9]{{1}} states the existence of a smooth
positive-definite metric on $(-1,1)^2$ for which no neighborhood of
the origin admits a smooth isometric immersion into $\mathbb{R}^3$.

The declaration `OAI.SmoothLocal.Geometry.exists_local_metric_without_local_immersion`
[S2:49–58]{{2}} in `IsometricImmersion/Main.lean` (68 lines, 0 `sorry`)
states and proves the counterexample: there exists a smooth positive
metric $g$ such that for every open neighborhood $U$ of the origin and
every smooth map $F : U \to \mathbb{R}^3$, the pullback metric condition
fails. The proof constructs an explicit counterexample metric and shows
no smooth immersion can pull it back.

The manuscript\u2019s patch obstruction proposition [S3:86–91]{{3}}
states that the metrics admitting no admissible height form a residual
subset, which is the stronger density result the Lean formalization
supports.
