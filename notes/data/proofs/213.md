## No infinite critical clusters on quasi-transitive graphs

**Partial (1 of 2).** The scope note [S1:10]{{1}} claims the
$\mathbb{Z}^3$ result and [S1:12]{{2}} claims the quasi-transitive
generalization. The `CriticalZ3` comparator is proven: the declaration
`OAI.CriticalZ3.critical_no_infinite` [S2:107–110]{{3}} in
`Main.lean` (115 lines, 0 `sorry`) states that at the bond and site
critical probabilities, every cluster is a.s. finite, by combining the
two individual theorems. The `CriticalPercolation` comparator
[S3:58]{{5}} has no `main_results` entry and its stub is
discharged by `sorry`.

The manuscript's main theorem [S4:17–22]{{4}} states the
$\mathbb{Z}^3$ result for both bond and site percolation. The Lean
proof matches this exactly. The companion paper on quasi-transitive
graphs (the scope note's second claim) is not formalized.
