## Exact Hausdorff gauges for SLE

**Not formalized.** The scope note claims [S1:10]{{1}} that
"The formalization proves" the positivity of the explicit gauge, but
the sole comparator `SLELowerPositivity.lean` is a `sorry` stub with no
`main_results` entry in `formalization.yaml`.

The companion paper proves two main theorems: the first (September 25)
establishes existence of a deterministic moment-integral gauge
[S3:38–47]{{4}}, and the second (September 26) identifies a regular
closed-form gauge $h(r)=r^d\log\log(1/r)^{p/2}$ and proves both
Hausdorff inequalities for it [S4:66–85]{{5}}. The comparator
`SourceLowerMainTarget` [S2:70–76]{{2}} encodes the lower
(positivity) bound for the regular gauge: every continuous nondecreasing
Hausdorff gauge agreeing with $h_\kappa$ at small radii assigns positive
measure to every nontrivial compact positive-time segment of ordinary
chordal SLE. The proof obligation is discharged by
`sorry` [S2:79]{{3}}.

The stub is notable for its rich type-level encoding: it defines
`IsOrdinaryChordalSLE` (a probabilistic condition with a Brownian-driven
Loewner flow), the gauge structure `IsGauge`, the Hausdorff measure
`hausdorffGauge`, and the segment predicate, before stating the target
as `SourceLowerMainTarget`. The carrier structure is complete and the
statement is faithful to the scope note; only the proof is absent.
