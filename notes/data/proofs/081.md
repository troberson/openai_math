## Riesz transforms and uniform rectifiability in higher codimension

**Match.** The manuscript's headline [S2:20]{2} is: an n-Ahlfors–David regular Radon measure on ℝ^d is uniformly n-rectifiable if its Riesz transform is uniformly bounded in L², for d ≥ 4 and 2 ≤ n ≤ d−2.
