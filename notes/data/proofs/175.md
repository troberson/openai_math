## Talagrand's expectation thresholds, discrete convexity, and graph decompositions

**Match.** Both comparator targets are proven as `main_results` entries.

The family spans two manuscripts and two results:

**Expectation threshold** [[3]]: q_f(ℱ) ≤ 25·512⁴·q(ℱ) for every nonempty proper increasing family on a finite nonempty ground set. The Lean declaration `talagrand_expectation_threshold_equivalence` [S2:21–24] [[1]] proves exactly this bound with the explicit constant 25·512⁴. The scope note [S1:10] records the same: cover budget 1/2, fractional covers may assign weights to every subset including the empty set.

**Discrete convexity** [[4]]: with k = 2⁷⁵, a family ℒ with Bernoulli-p measure ≥ 1 − 1/k has its exceptional set E_k(ℒ) p-small (containment cover of total p-cost ≤ 1/2). The Lean declaration `talagrand_discrete_convexity` [S3:61–65] [[2]] proves `Small p (exceptional (2^75) D)` from `familyMeasure p D ≥ 1 − 1/2^75`, delegating to `original_density_small`. The scope note [S1:12] records the same with k = 2⁷⁵, no monotonicity assumption, and repeated members allowed.

The two comparators [S1:18–19] are the shared targets.
