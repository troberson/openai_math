**Partial.**

> “The paper’s main conclusion is an actual isomorphism

classification $A\otimes W\otimes K \cong B\otimes W\otimes K$,
which the Lean formalization does not establish. The Lean
proves a supporting component of the trace-cone machinery,
not the main theorem.
