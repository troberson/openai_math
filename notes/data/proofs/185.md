## Infinite matroid packing/covering and intersection counterexamples

**Partial.** 1 of 2 comparator targets is proven as a `main_results` entry.

The preprint's main theorem [[2]] states that in ZFC, there exist a countably infinite set E and two self-dual infinite matroids M₀, M₁ on E such that no independent sets I₀ ∈ M₀, I₁ ∈ M₁ cover E, and the pair has no packing/covering partition. The scope note [S1:9] records the same: two self-dual partitional matroids on one countably infinite ground set admitting neither an independent covering nor a packing/covering partition.

The Lean declaration `main` [S2:12–18] [[1]] proves the six-conjunct statement: E is countably infinite, M₀ and M₁ are self-dual matroids on E, no independent pair covers E, and no packing/covering partition exists. The proof constructs the matroids from a concrete rank structure via `exists_concrete_rank` and `exists_basisSystem`, then builds the self-dual matroid Q on the ultrafilter structure.

The scope note [S1:11] also records separate formalized consequences: counterexamples to the individual covering and packing conjectures, and the refutation of the unrestricted infinite matroid intersection conjecture. The `InfiniteMatroidCorollaries` comparator [S1:18] has **no `main_results` entry** and is a 129-line `sorry` stub.
