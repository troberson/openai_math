## Overview

Two new manuscripts with Lean formalizations, one new formalization of an existing family, three
new comparator configs, and a restoration of C++ verification data. The manuscript count moves
**719 → 721**, the family count moves **372 → 373** (one new family; the second new manuscript
joins an existing family's overview entry as a companion). No withdrawals.

The two headline results are notable for their scope: one proves Sogge's local smoothing conjecture
for the Euclidean wave equation in every dimension $n \ge 2$ (with the Kakeya and Nikodym maximal
conjectures as consequences), and the other gives a deterministic subexponential-time algorithm for
$fk$-CNF counting that refutes the Exponential Time Hypothesis for 3-SAT. The release notes that an
"Independent working group of computer scientists led by Russell Impagliazzo, Ramamohan Paturi, and
Ryan Williams will examine and explain the proof" of the ETH paper.

lengths."*
