## Overview

One sign error in one argument withdrew three manuscripts. In the stabilization-trace argument for
negative double points the proof gave the reverse traces sign `+1`; the two branches of the standard
cusp carry opposite source orientations, so each reverse trace contributes `-1`, the signed count
after stabilization is `-2m` and not zero, and the Eliashberg–Murphy cancellation theorem the proof
invokes at that step needs a zero signed count. Two of the three papers were built on that
construction, so they went with it. The release then repaired 14 manuscripts, re-issued 13 more for
citations alone, and added 11 formalizations — six of headline results, five of supporting results,
by its own split. The manuscript count moves **722 → 719** and the family count holds at **372**.
The release states its coverage as *"This brings the total percentage of top-line results formalized
to 300 / 719 = ~42%."* (`history.md:31`) — that is the release's claim; our ledger measures a
different quantity and reads 172 formalized manuscripts of the 719 listed.

## The withdrawal

The release names one cause (`history.md:9`) — *"In “Algebraicity of Weil classes on split abelian
eightfolds” a sign error invalidates a stabilization-trace cancellation argument and the
construction used by two dependent papers."* — and lists the three (`history.md:11`):

```
- Algebraicity of Weil classes on split abelian eightfolds
- Algebraicity of Kuga–Satake Correspondences for K3 Surfaces
- The rational Hodge conjecture for products of K3 surfaces
```

All three are family **032**. Each was withdrawn on October 6, the day before the commit
(`preprints/Algebraicity-of-Weil-classes-on-split-abelian-eightfolds-September-18-2026/README.md:4`
— *"**Withdrawn on October 6, 2026.**"*).

The mechanism is stated in full in the eightfolds notice. The convention and the claim
(`preprints/Algebraicity-of-Weil-classes-on-split-abelian-eightfolds-September-18-2026/README.md:6`)
— *"In the manuscript's signed double-point convention, write $I(f_1)=-m$ with $m>0$. The proof
assigns each reverse stabilization trace sign $+1$ and therefore claims that inserting $m$ such
traces makes the signed count zero."* The correction (`…README.md:8`) — *"Accounting for the opposite
source orientations of the two branches of the standard cusp gives sign $-1$ for each reverse trace
in this convention. The resulting count is therefore"*:

`preprints/Algebraicity-of-Weil-classes-on-split-abelian-eightfolds-September-18-2026/README.md:10`

```
$$I_{\mathrm{new}}=I(f_1)-m=-2m\ne0.$$
```

and the consequence (`…README.md:12`) — *"The Eliashberg-Murphy cancellation theorem invoked at this
step requires zero signed double-point count, so its hypothesis is not met. The subsequent
oriented-surgery and embedded-brane construction is therefore unsupported, and the paper does not
establish its claimed algebraicity theorem."*

The two dependents say the same about themselves
(`preprints/Algebraicity-of-Kuga-Satake-Correspondences-for-K3-Surfaces-October-3-2026/README.md:6`)
— *"The proof relies on an adaptation of the flawed stabilization-trace construction in [Algebraicity
of Weil classes on split abelian eightfolds](…). The sign error in that construction prevents the
required signed-double-point cancellation. The resulting gap leaves this paper's claimed algebraicity
theorem unproved."* and
(`preprints/The-rational-Hodge-conjecture-for-products-of-K3-surfaces-October-4-2026/README.md:6`) —
*"The resulting gap leaves this paper's claimed rational Hodge theorem for products of projective K3
surfaces unproved."*

This is a proof withdrawal, not a refutation, and the release says so
(`preprints/Algebraicity-of-Weil-classes-on-split-abelian-eightfolds-September-18-2026/README.md:14`)
— *"This withdrawal concerns the proof; it does not assert that the mathematical statement is
false."* The same sentence closes all three notices
(`preprints/Algebraicity-of-Kuga-Satake-Correspondences-for-K3-Surfaces-October-3-2026/README.md:8`).
Each notice pins the pre-withdrawal PDF in the repository at
`adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a` (`…eightfolds-September-18-2026/README.md:19`).
